## Original Audit Weakness

Southern Pecan Gulf Coast Kitchen is an established culinary destination situated in the upscale Phillips Place shopping center in Charlotte’s SouthPark district at 6706-C Phillips Place Court. Specializing in from-scratch Gulf Coast, Lowcountry, and Cajun-Creole dishes such as Charleston Shrimp & Grits, Seafood & Andouille Gumbo, Blackened Redfish, and homemade Southern Pecan Pie, it represents genuine coastal Southern hospitality. However, third-party listings often generalize it as a generic seafood grill or fail to detail its daily scratch kitchen preparations, Lowcountry sourcing, or private dining accommodations.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **SouthPark Phillips Place Location:** Located at 6706-C Phillips Place Court, Charlotte NC 28210. Phone: `(704) 749-2949`.
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Open seven days a week: Daily 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
3. **From-Scratch Coastal Southern Culinary Lineup:** Charleston Shrimp & Grits with andouille sausage, slow-simmered Seafood Gumbo, Blackened Redfish with crawfish cream, and golden fried Gulf oysters.
4. **Signature Southern Scratch Desserts:** Warm homemade Southern Pecan Pie with toasted pecans and flaky butter crust, peach cobbler, and sticky toffee butter cake.
5. **Phillips Place Hospitality:** Sophisticated dining room, European-style courtyard patio, and dedicated private dining room for family and corporate gatherings.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Lowcountry Cypress & Warm Pecan Palette:** Deep coastal cypress obsidian (`#0e1311`), rich roasted pecan card (`#181e19`), warm buttered pecan gold (`#d4973b`), marshland sage green (`#52796f`), and linen chalk white (`#f7f8f5`).
2. **Refined Coastal Southern Typography:** Elegant display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced kitchen notes (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Lowcountry Culinary Photography:** Creamy shrimp and stone-ground grits with andouille gravy and crumbled bacon, slow-simmered seafood and andouille gumbo over white rice, and freshly baked Southern pecan pie slice on ceramic plate.
