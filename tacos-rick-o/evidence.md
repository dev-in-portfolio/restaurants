## Original Audit Weakness

Tacos Rick-O is Charlotte's viral Latin street food truck and catering kitchen founded by chef-owner Ricky Ortiz. Celebrated across the Queen City for its signature 12-hour braised Jalisco-style beef birria quesatacos with rich consommé dip, the showstopping "Ricko Platter" (birria tacos, crispy seasoned wings, sweet fried plantains, and queso fries), Hot Cheeto birria balls, and corporate taco bar catering, its current website is built on an entry-level basic builder with minimal interactive navigation, fragmented schedule information, and missing culinary storytelling.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Birria Crimson & Sunfire Agave Amber Palette:** Deep Obsidian Charcoal (`#141212`), Aztec Dark Clay (`#1f1816`), Card Grill Panel (`#2c201d`), Birria Crimson (`#c82333`), Sunfire Agave Amber (`#f39c12`), and Warm Tortilla Masa Cream (`#faf6f0`).
2. **Bold Street-Style Display Typography:** Display sans (*Oswald* / *Playfair Display*) paired with clean geometric body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and kitchen ticket monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Floating Hero Badge:** Concrete operational facts (`Tacos Rick-O | Charlotte Premium Outlets • Chef Ricky Ortiz`).
4. **Editorial Layout:** Three-card standards grid without emojis, followed by dual alternating highlight banners (`.ricko-highlight-banner`, `.reverse`).
