## Original Audit Weakness

Sabores de Oaxaca is a groundbreaking regional culinary concept created by the revered family behind Charlotte's landmark Tacos El Nevado. Located along the bustling Central Avenue international corridor in East Charlotte, it brings rare Oaxacan dishes (such as giant 14-inch tlayudas, complex mole negro, and handmade memelas) to the city. However, as a new establishment opened in May 2026, it lacked an authoritative owned digital presence. Third-party portals frequently conflated it with commercial chains like Sabor Latin Street Grill or lacked essential details regarding its generational Oaxacan recipes and operating hours.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Central Avenue Location:** 3654 Central Ave, Charlotte NC 28205. Anchoring the diverse Central Avenue corridor in East Charlotte.
2. **Authentic Oaxacan Gastronomic Program:** Hand-toasted giant 14-inch Tlayudas (tasajo, cecina enchilada, chorizo, quesillo string cheese, asiento, avocado), complex Mole Negro Oaxaqueño (chilhuacle chilies, Mexican chocolate, spices), Sopes, Memelas con hoja de aguacate, Torta de Pierna, and traditional Chocolate Oaxaqueño frothed with pan de yema.
3. **Lineage & Operating Hours:** Founded by the culinary family behind Tacos El Nevado; Phone (980) 207-2581; Open Tuesday – Sunday: 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Monday Closed).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Oaxacan Terracotta & Cacao Noir Palette:** Deep artisanal Oaxacan clay terracotta (`#b84a29`), dark cacao mole noir (`#18100e`), agave green (`#2d4a3e`), golden maize yellow (`#d99b26`), and raw parchment linen (`#fcfaf6`).
2. **Bold Heritage Typography:** Architectural, handcrafted display typography (*Cinzel* and *Plus Jakarta Sans*) evoking Mesoamerican stone carving and vibrant Oaxacan hand-painted market signage.
3. **Authentic Oaxacan Photography:** Giant crispy tlayuda on woven tablecloth with shredded quesillo and avocado, rich obsidian Mole Negro over poultry with Mexican rice, and hot frothed chocolate in an artisanal clay bowl with sweet pan de yema.
