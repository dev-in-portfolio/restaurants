## Original Audit Weakness

Vibe Café & Wine Bar is an authentic European bistro, morning espresso bar, and evening wine lounge located at 1315 East Boulevard (Suite 110) in Charlotte's historic Dilworth neighborhood. The cafe offers a unique culinary fusion of Eastern European / Slavic scratch comfort (including cottage cheese syrniki, traditional slow-simmered beet borscht, handmade meat pelmeni, golden meat cheburek, and multi-layered Medovik honey cake) alongside Mediterranean staples (Roman holiday flatbreads, burrata caprese, hummus, and Old World estate wines). Despite its extraordinary scratch cooking and charming interior with living green walls and art murals, its online presence lacked a cohesive multi-page showcase that highlighted its full culinary range from morning syrniki breakfast to late-night wine pairings.
