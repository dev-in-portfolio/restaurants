## Original Audit Weakness

Sea Level NC is Uptown Charlotte's premier coastal seafood destination and raw bar, located at 129 East 5th Street near the bustling corner of 5th and College. Renowned for their proprietary farm-to-fork "Sea Level Salts" oysters harvested directly from Core Sound in Sea Level, North Carolina, their legendary weekday $1.50 oyster happy hour, and fresh Maine lobster rolls, online directory profiles frequently reduce their brand to generic seafood listings without highlighting their direct coastal farm partnerships, sustainable sourcing integrity, or Truist Tower parking validation.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Uptown Charlotte Flagship:** Located at 129 East 5th St, Charlotte NC 28202 (Heart of Uptown Charlotte). Phone: (704) 412-2616. Email: `sealevel@eat704.com`.
2. **Proprietary Sea Level Salts Oysters:** Farmed directly in Core Sound (Sea Level, NC) with brisk oceanic salinity and a sweet, clean finish. Served on crushed ice with lemon and house mignonette.
3. **Daily Oyster Happy Hour:** Monday – Friday, 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM featuring $1.50 house oysters.
4. **Maine Lobster Rolls & Coastal Classics:** Split-top toasted brioche buns stuffed with claw and knuckle lobster meat, Carolina Shrimp & Grits with andouille pan gravy, and blackened Atlantic catches.
5. **Truist Tower Parking Validation:** Validated parking in the adjacent Truist Tower garage (up to 2 hours before 6:00 PM, up to 3 hours after 6:00 PM).
6. **Operating Schedule:** Mon–Sat: 11:30 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight), Dining room open until 10:00 PM Mon–Thu and 11:00 PM Fri–Sat. Sunday Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Maritime Raw Bar Navy & Coastal Pearl Palette:** Deep Atlantic oceanic navy (`#091118`), raw bar slate card (`#121d28`), sea glass teal (`#2a8f8d`), oyster shell pearl gold (`#d4af37`), and crisp sea-salt white (`#f4f8fb`).
2. **Refined Nautical Editorial Typography:** Elegant serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced farm/salinity indicators (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Raw Bar & Coastal Dish Photography:** Freshly shucked raw oysters on crushed ice with lemon and parsley, overflowing Maine lobster roll on toasted brioche, and pan-seared Carolina shrimp with creamy grits.
