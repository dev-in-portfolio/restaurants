## Original Audit Weakness

The Thirsty Beaver Saloon is Charlotte’s most legendary and internationally celebrated honky-tonk dive bar, nestled at 1225 Central Avenue in Plaza Midwood. Opened in 2008 by brothers Brian and Mark Wilson on property owned by George Salem, the saloon achieved national fame when its owners and landlord refused multiple buyout offers from developers, forcing a 323-unit luxury apartment complex to be constructed directly around the tiny 1,000-square-foot cinderblock bar in a dramatic horseshoe. It gained further global notoriety in September 2021 when Mick Jagger stopped by for a beer on the patio, unnoticed by surrounding patrons. Despite its cult status, the Beaver has maintained no formal digital presence beyond scattered social media tags and outdated directory snippets, leaving visitors searching for clear operational hours, cash-handling policies, and parking logistics.

3. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Open 12:00 PM – 2:00 AM daily, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
4. **The Iconic Holdout Legacy:** The famous image of the tiny brick saloon surrounded on three sides by multi-story apartment construction; the orange side wall featuring the cowboy-hat-wearing beaver mural; and the yellow wooden saloon sign with the green shamrock.
5. **No-Frills Honky-Tonk Ethos:** Ice-cold domestic cans and longneck bottles (PBR, Miller High Life, Lonestar), shot-and-a-beer combos, local North Carolina craft taps, legendary coin-operated jukebox, and BYO outside food policy.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Defiant Saloon Neon & Weathered Brick Palette:** Raw cinderblock charcoal (`#141312`), weathered tavern brick (`#a3432b`), saloon gold ochre (`#e5a93b`), shamrock green (`#27633c`), and neon beer sign glow (`#ffb03a`).
2. **True Honky-Tonk Saloon Typography:** Bold display serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean utilitarian sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and tavern receipt monospace (*DM Mono*).
3. **Verified Real Historical Photography:** The historic photograph of the saloon standing firm during the apartment construction wrap; the side view showing the beaver mascot mural in boots and cowboy hat; and the hand-carved yellow wooden entrance sign hung with warm incandescent string lights.
