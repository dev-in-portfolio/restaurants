🍴 Authentic Horn of Africa Cuisine • East Charlotte

Communal Feast, Ancient Spices.

Charlotte’s landmark destination for authentic Eritrean and Ethiopian dining. Gather around the traditional woven messob basket to tear warm, fermented sourdough teff injera and scoop up rich berbere-simmered wats, sizzling skillet tibs, and vibrant vegan combo platters at 4301 Monroe Road.
