## Original Audit Weakness
The restaurant’s active web presence (`adamarysrestauranteypupuserianc.com`) is a generic white-label ordering template using basic Bootstrap defaults with dark uninspired overlays. It lacks warm Salvadoran art direction, editorial food photography, storytelling around Central Ave's vibrant Latin food scene, and structured catering guidance.

## Core Design Response
Created a bespoke premium Salvadoran core website built around warm terracotta (`#C05A3E`), deep agave green (`#2C5E43`), and maize gold (`#E5A93C`) tones. Paired elegant Playfair Display serifs with clean Plus Jakarta Sans typography. Built six substantive linked pages highlighting the artisanal hand-patted pupusa process, traditional breakfasts, party platter catering options, and practical visit details.

## Omitted Uncertain Facts

- Omitted exact individual pricing fluctuations to prevent stale price promises.
- Omitted exact parking stall counts.

## QA Results & Technical Verification
- **Substantive Pages (6):** `index.html`, `menu.html`, `story.html`, `pupusas.html`, `catering.html`, `visit.html`.
- **Navigation & Links:** Tested full navigation loop; every page links to all other pages and returns seamlessly to home.
- **Mobile Responsiveness:** Tested viewports down to 360px width; verified zero horizontal overflow, clean mobile hamburger navigation drawer, and proper touch targets (>= 44px).
- **Keyboard & Accessibility:** Verified visible focus outlines (`outline: 3px solid #E5A93C`), skip to content links, proper ARIA attributes (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label`), and `prefers-reduced-motion` CSS overrides.
- **Safety & Non-Deceptive Forms:** All demo forms use non-submitting event handlers with clear demo disclaimer toasts pointing users to the official phone number (704) 666-8986.
- **Console Errors & Placeholders:** Zero console errors; zero lorem ipsum; zero template residue.
