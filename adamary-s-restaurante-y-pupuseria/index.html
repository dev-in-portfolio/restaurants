Tradition & Quality

Hand-Shaped Masa, Fresh Curtido & Salsa Roja

A true pupusa is defined by its crust and filling harmony. At Adamary’s, every pupusa is patted by hand upon order and cooked on a hot griddle until crisp on the outside and gooey within.

Paired with our house-fermented curtido (tangy cabbage and carrot slaw) and mild tomato salsa, it is the heart of Salvadoran street food culture right here in East Charlotte.
