## Original Audit Weakness

Freshwaters is one of Uptown Charlotte's most cherished Black-owned culinary landmarks, drawing diners from across the region for Clifton and Sharon Freshwater's authentic New Orleans Creole recipes and Sunday soul dinners. However, their online footprint suffered from fragmented social media listings and an outdated web design that failed to convey their warm French Quarter dining room aesthetic, scratch-cooking philosophy, and private catering services. This 6-page concept provides an elegant, authoritative digital home honoring their culinary legacy.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **New Orleans Creole Seafood:** Crispy cornmeal-crusted golden fried catfish, crawfish étouffée over fluffy white rice, dark roux seafood gumbo, and Louisiana seafood boils packed with snow crab clusters, jumbo shrimp, and andouille sausage.
2. **Heritage Southern Soul Classics:** Slow-smoked St. Louis BBQ ribs, smothered pork chops, slow-simmered collard greens with smoked turkey, four-cheese baked macaroni, and candied sweet potatoes.
3. **Scratch-Baked Desserts:** Famous warm Southern peach cobbler with flaky golden crust, sweet potato pie, and powdered sugar French Quarter beignets.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Bourbon Street Mahogany & French Quarter Brass Palette:** Bourbon Street Mahogany (`#3d1e11`), French Quarter Brass Gold (`#d4a373`), Cajun Cayenne (`#b23a22`), and Linen Parchment (`#faf7f2`).
2. **Uptown Bistro Visual Architecture:** Rich Creole parlor ambiance, dark roux simmering callouts, and Sunday soul dinner schedules.
3. **Opulent Editorial Typography:** Playfair Display elegant serif paired with Plus Jakarta Sans body and DM Mono for clear entree courses and pricing.
