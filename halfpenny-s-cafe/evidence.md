## Original Audit Weakness

Halfpenny’s Cafe has served Uptown Charlotte’s banking and business community for years with comforting diner hospitality and fast breakfast and lunch service. However, their digital footprint was limited to third-party listing snippets lacking an interactive all-day breakfast griddle guide, office catering platters, and espresso bar details. This 6-page concept provides a warm, energetic digital storefront for the cafe.

---

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **All-Day Breakfast Biscuits & Omelets:** Fresh baked flaky buttermilk biscuits, toasted croissants, and bagels stuffed with fried eggs, thick-cut bacon, country sausage, and melted cheese, plus 3-egg griddle omelets with crispy hash browns.
2. **Deli Lunch Paninis & Scratch Soups:** Fresh roasted turkey clubs, hot pastrami on rye, tuna melts on sourdough, chef salads, and daily homemade soups.
3. **Morning Espresso & Afternoon Ice Cream:** Fresh brewed Colombian roasts, chai tea lattes, and hand-dipped ice cream cones.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Bronze & Morning Cream Palette:** Copper Halfpenny Bronze (`#b45309`), Morning Cream White (`#faf8f5`), Warm Pecan Gold (`#d97706`), and Two Wells Slate Charcoal (`#1e293b`).
2. **Diner Visual Architecture:** All-day breakfast matrices, deli panini build callouts, and office meeting catering packages.
3. **Warm Modern Typography:** Syne display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for morning speed and clarity.
