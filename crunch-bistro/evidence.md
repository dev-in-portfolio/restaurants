## Original Audit Weakness

Crunch Bistro is Uptown Charlotte’s beloved independent destination for fresh, nutrient-dense weekday lunches, offering over 40 fresh ingredients and chef-crafted dressings. However, the restaurant lacks an owned independent website, leaving Uptown corporate workers and visitors to rely on fragmented delivery platform links with missing ingredient stories, unformatted catering guides, and absent nutritional breakdowns. This 6-page concept establishes a vibrant digital home celebrating clean eating, customizable leaf bases, and Uptown pickup convenience.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Under-500 Calorie Clean Eating:** Most entrees crafted under 500 calories without sacrificing flavor, featuring 40+ toppings, organic quinoa, and house-made scratch dressings.
2. **Signature Bistro Favorites:** Asian Crunch Salad with almonds and mandarin oranges, Barbecue Santa Fe with grilled BBQ chicken and black beans, Turkey Cobb with avocado and bleu cheese, Warm Quinoa Bowls, Margherita Lavash Flatbread, and Crunch Chicken Wrap.
3. **Uptown Charlotte Core Anchor:** Located at 401 N Tryon St, serving corporate towers, Fourth Ward residents, and Center City commuters Monday through Friday.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Fresh Botanical & Citrus Palette:** Fresh Leaf Emerald (`#2d6a4f`), Citrus Tangerine (`#f77f00`), Clean Porcelain (`#fcfbf9`), and Slate Charcoal (`#1d2024`).
2. **Pick-Your-Leaf & Customizer Architecture:** Visual ingredient matrices, step-by-step leaf selection (Romaine, Baby Spinach, Kale, Arugula), and dietary tags (GF, V, DF).
3. **Clean Modern Typography:** Modern geometric Outfit display paired with Plus Jakarta Sans body and DM Mono for exact calorie and macro callouts.
