Nuestra Historia y Tradición

Bringing authentic Salvadoran pupusas and comfort food home to Central Avenue in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Rooted in Heritage

From El Salvador to Central Avenue

For generations in El Salvador, the pupusa has been more than a dish—it is a communal ritual. From street corners in Olocuilta to family kitchens, the rhythm of slapping corn masa by hand signifies warmth, welcome, and home.

Located at 3100 Central Avenue in East Charlotte, Adamary’s Restaurante y Pupuseria was founded to preserve and share these authentic culinary traditions with our local community.

Every morning, our kitchen prepares fresh corn masa, simmers seasoned beans and pork chicharrón, and chops tender loroco buds to ensure every customer tastes true Salvadoran craft.

Our Kitchen Pillars

What Makes Adamary’s Special

Fresh Comal Cooking

We never pre-make or heat lamps our pupusas. Each order is slapped by hand and cooked immediately on the hot flat griddle until golden and crisp.

Authentic Ingredients

From imported Loroco flower buds to house-made cultured crema and red beans, we source authentic ingredients that preserve true Central American flavor.

Family Hospitality

Whether you are stopping in for a quick weekday lunch, a hearty Sunday breakfast with family, or late night pupusas, you are treated like family.
