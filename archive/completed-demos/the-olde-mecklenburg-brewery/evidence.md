## Original Audit Weakness

The Olde Mecklenburg Brewery (OMB) is Charlotte’s foundational craft brewing institution and the birthplace of the Queen City’s craft beer revolution, founded in 2009 by John Marrino. Operating its flagship 8-acre biergarten and Brauhaus at 4150 Yancey Road in Lower South End (LoSo) alongside its Ballantyne destination at 15025 Bowl Street, OMB is world-renowned for strictly adhering to the 1516 German Reinheitsgebot (Purity Law)—brewing exclusively with pure water, malted barley, Bavarian noble hops, and proprietary yeast. Despite international medal wins for its flagship Copper Altbier and Captain Jack Pilsner, third-party aggregators often present OMB as a standard American sports pub, flattening its authentic Bavarian Brauhaus culinary heritage (such as scratch bratwurst platters, salted Laugenbrezeln, and crispy schnitzel), its sprawling Munich-style open-air tree canopy, and its strict Reinheitsgebot brewing ethos into generic text.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Flagship LoSo & Ballantyne Campuses:** 4150 Yancey Rd, Charlotte, NC 28217 (Lower South End Flagship) and 15025 Bowl St, Charlotte, NC 28277 (Ballantyne).
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Monday – Thursday 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM; Friday – Saturday 11:00 AM – 11:00 PM; Sunday 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM.
3. **Reinheitsgebot 1516 Purity Standards:** Four ingredients only: pure water, two-row malted barley, hallertau noble hops, and bottom/top fermenting yeast cultures with zero adjuncts, preservatives, or artificial additives.
4. **Signature Brews:** Copper Düsseldorf-Style Altbier (Charlotte's iconic amber ale), Captain Jack Munich-Style Pilsner, Hornet's Nest Bavarian Hefeweizen, and 70 Knots IPA.
5. **Authentic Brauhaus Fare:** Grilled Bavarian bratwurst with warm braised sauerkraut, pan-fried German potatoes, and sweet mustard; freshly baked coarse-salted Bavarian pretzels (*Laugenbrezeln*) with warm beer cheese dip; and breaded crisp pork schnitzel with lemon and lingonberries.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Bavarian Brauhaus & Copper Brewhouse Palette:** Copper kettle amber (`#c46d29`), Brauhaus timber brown (`#221a14`), deep malt obsidian (`#14100d`), Bavarian sky azure (`#2e6b9e`), golden malt froth (`#f5b842`), and Bavarian cream linen (`#f7f4ee`).
2. **German Brewery & Heritage Typography:** Regal traditional serif (*UnifrakturMaguntia* / *Cinzel Decorative* / *Cinzel*) paired with clean structural sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and brewing specification monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A plate of sizzling grilled bratwurst with rich pan jus, braised sauerkraut, roasted potatoes, mustard, and a glass of amber Altbier on a wooden brewery table; an authentic coarse-salted Bavarian pretzel with sweet mustard and Weißwurst; and towering copper brewing kettles inside an active brewhouse.
