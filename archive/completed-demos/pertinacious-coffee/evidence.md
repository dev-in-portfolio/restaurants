## Original Audit Weakness

Pertinacious Coffee relies on basic Square template order pages and fragmented directory links. Located in vibrant South End on Graham St, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its micro-lot roasting craft, house-made botanical syrup lab, mobile espresso cart event catering, and South End morning directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Charlotte Location:** 1200 S Graham St, Charlotte NC 28203 (South End / Gold District / The Studio).
2. **Specialty Coffee & Syrup Specialties:** Single-Origin Precision Espresso, House-Made Syrups (Brown Sugar Cardamom, Bourbon Vanilla, Lavender Honey), Nitro & Slow Cold Brew, Ceremonial Matcha Lattes, and Artisanal Morning Pastries.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (336) 480-6299; Mon–Fri 6:30 AM – 3:00 PM | Sat–Sun 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Contemporary Geometric Editorial Sans Typography:** Expressive geometric sans (*Epilogue*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and roastery batch ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Espresso Crema & Warm Oat Cream Palette:** Modern specialty roastery palette anchored in warm espresso crema dark (`#1C1917`), toasted oat cream (`#F5EBE1`), bright tangerine blossom (`#EA580C`), caramel roast (`#C2410C`), and clean sand (`#FAFAF9`).
3. **"The Micro-Lot Espresso & House Syrup Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`single-origin-espresso-and-roastery-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing single-origin espresso pulls and scratch syrups alongside slow-drip cold brew, ceremonial matcha, and fresh pastries.

### Divergent Choices for Pertinacious Coffee

- **Hero Composition:** Modern South End Specialty Roastery & Espresso Lab split-hero layout: left side features an Artisan Coffee Lab Seal badge (`"ARTISANAL ESPRESSO & HOUSE SYRUP LAB • SOUTH END CHARLOTTE"`), contemporary geometric sans typography (*Epilogue*), warm espresso crema dark & oat cream palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of precision pour-over and latte art.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Pertinacious Vault Cards** (`pertinacious-vault-card`) and South End specialty coffee bar highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`pertinacious-header`, `pertinacious-brand`, `roastery-hero-stage`, `pertinacious-seal-badge`, `pertinacious-vault-card`, `pertinacious-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
