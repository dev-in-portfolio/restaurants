## Original Audit Weakness

Pho Huong Que relies on third-party food delivery listings and minimal online footprint. Located on busy E Independence Blvd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 24-hour slow-simmered star anise and charred ginger bone broth technique, herb garden garnishes, vermicelli noodle bowls, and dining room directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **East Independence Blvd Charlotte Location:** 7211 E Independence Blvd, Ste 13, Charlotte NC 28227.
2. **Vietnamese Culinary Specialties:** Phở Đặc Biệt (House Special Combination Beef Pho with rare steak, flank, tendon, and tripe), Bún Bò Huế (Spicy Hue Lemongrass Beef Noodle Soup), Crispy Chả Giò Pork & Shrimp Egg Rolls, Bún Thịt Nướng (Grilled Pork Vermicelli with Nuoc Cham), and Cà Phê Sữa Đá (Vietnamese Slow-Drip Iced Coffee).
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 536-0401; Fri–Wed 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Closed Thursdays.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Organic Literary Display Serif Typography:** Expressive editorial serif (*Newsreader*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Saigon kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Saigon Forest Emerald & Star Anise Amber Palette:** Earthy Vietnamese culinary palette anchored in deep Saigon forest emerald (`#064E3B`), rich Vietnamese jade (`#022C22`), star anise amber (`#D97706`), and fresh thai basil mint (`#10B981`).
3. **"The 24-Hour Bone Broth Pho & Bún Vermicelli Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`bone-broth-pho-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing 24-hour simmered beef bone broths and vermicelli noodle bowls alongside crispy appetizers and slow-drip coffee.

- **Hero Composition:** Vietnamese Saigon Heritage & 24-Hour Bone Broth Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Vietnamese Pho Herb Stamp badge (`"24-HOUR SIMMERED BONE BROTH • CHARLOTTE NC"`), organic literary display serif typography (*Newsreader*), deep emerald & star anise amber palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of steaming beef pho with fresh thai basil, bean sprouts, and lime wedges.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Huong Vault Cards** (`huong-vault-card`) and East Independence Vietnamese culinary highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`huong-header`, `huong-brand`, `saigon-hero-stage`, `huong-seal-badge`, `huong-vault-card`, `huong-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
