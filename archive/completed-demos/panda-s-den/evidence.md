## Original Audit Weakness

Panda’s Den relies on basic aggregator menu listings and unbranded takeout portals. Located at 5724 E W.T. Harris Blvd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its intense high-heat wok culinary technique, scratch sauces, signature Chinese classics, and direct takeout ordering directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **East Charlotte NC Location:** 5724 E W.T. Harris Blvd, Charlotte NC 28215.
2. **Master Wok-Fired Specialties:** Crispy General Tso's Chicken, Honey Glazed Sesame Chicken, House Special Lo Mein (pork, chicken, shrimp, vegetables), Szechuan Spicy Beef with Broccoli, Crispy Crab Rangoon, Steamed Pork Dumplings, and Hot & Sour Soup.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 567-0088; Mon–Sun 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Open 7 Days a Week).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Majestic Imperial Display Serif Typography:** Stately imperial display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and wok station kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Imperial Lacquer Vermilion & Wok-Fire Amber Palette:** Dramatic Asian culinary palette anchored in imperial lacquer vermilion (`#881337`), wok-fire glowing amber (`#F59E0B`), bamboo gold (`#D97706`), and steamed dumpling cream (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Wok-Fired Specialties & Dim Sum Appetizers Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`wok-fired-mastery-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing flaming wok classics alongside dim sum starters and family combination platters.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Olivelli Deli` — Modern Italian Salumeria with Syne fonts and Olive Green/Calabrian Chili.
2. `Oh My Soul` — Afro-Bohemian Botanical Sanctuary with Outfit fonts and Jungle Forest Green/Marigold.
3. `Oh My Ganache, The Cheesecake Bar` — Modern Artisan Confectionery with Playfair Display fonts and Dark Cocoa/Raspberry Rose.

### Divergent Choices for Panda’s Den

- **Hero Composition:** Master Wok-Fired Szechuan Dragon Vault & Imperial Chinese Kitchen split-hero layout: left side features an Imperial Wok Seal badge (`"MASTER WOK-FIRED CHINESE KITCHEN & TAKEOUT • CHARLOTTE NC"`), majestic imperial display typography (*Cinzel*), deep imperial lacquer vermilion & wok-fire amber palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of wok-fired poultry and chef sauté action.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Panda Vault Cards** (`panda-vault-card`) and Charlotte East Harris Blvd takeout highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`panda-header`, `panda-brand`, `wok-hero-stage`, `panda-seal-badge`, `panda-vault-card`, `panda-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
