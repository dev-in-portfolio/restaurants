## Original Audit Weakness

Platform Coffee + Kitchen relies on generic Toast ordering links and third-party coffee directory pages. Located in the Station West district in Wesley Heights, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its precision micro-roasting craft, all-day kitchen menu, high-speed remote work amenities, and Station West pickup directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Station West Wesley Heights Location:** 919 Berryhill Rd #104, Charlotte NC 28208.
2. **Roastery & Kitchen Specialties:** House-Roasted Single-Origin Pour-Overs, Nitro Cold Brew, Golden Turmeric & Lavender Lattes, Sourdough Avocado Tartines, Chorizo Breakfast Burritos, Pressed Paninis, and House Scratch Pastries.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 916-9191; Mon–Sat 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM | Sun Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Architectural Tech Sans Typography:** Forward-looking geometric display sans (*Space Grotesk*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Station West roasting log mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Industrial Railway Slate & Copper Amber Palette:** Modern industrial palette anchored in deep railway iron (`#18181B`), roastery carbon slate (`#09090B`), micro-roast copper amber (`#D97706`), fresh botanical sage (`#10B981`), warm steamed oat cream (`#F5EBE1`), and clean linen white.
3. **"The Micro-Roasting & Artisan Kitchen Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`specialty-roastery-and-kitchen-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing micro-roasted single-origin coffees and espresso flights alongside breakfast burritos, sourdough tartines, and scratch pastries.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Pho Quynh` — Vietnamese Saigon Heritage with Newsreader and Saigon Cinnamon/Broth Amber.
2. `Poppy’s Bagels & More` — NYC Kettle Bakery with Fraunces and Brooklyn Navy/Deli Mustard.

### Divergent Choices for Platform Coffee + Kitchen

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Industrial Railway Roastery & Station West Workspace split-hero layout: left side features a Station West Roastery Seal badge (`"SPECIALTY MICRO-ROASTERY & ALL-DAY KITCHEN • STATION WEST WESLEY HEIGHTS"`), clean architectural sans typography (*Space Grotesk*), railway slate & copper amber palette, and right side features a framed hero image of artisanal espresso pours and specialty lattes.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Platform Vault Cards** (`platform-vault-card`) and Wesley Heights roastery highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`platform-header`, `platform-brand`, `station-hero-stage`, `platform-seal-badge`, `platform-vault-card`, `platform-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
