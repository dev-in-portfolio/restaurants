## Original Audit Weakness

XOXO Cocktail Lounge is Uptown Charlotte’s premier high-energy boutique cocktail lounge, communal punch bowl destination, and VIP table venue located at 314 N College St. Despite its massive weekend draw, VIP reservations, and upscale clientele, the venue’s existing web presence shared a single landing page with Encore Nightclub, suffering from sparse cocktail details, missing interactive multi-page VIP discovery, and slow content delivery.
