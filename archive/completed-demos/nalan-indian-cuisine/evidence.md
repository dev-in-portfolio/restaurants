## Original Audit Weakness

Nalan Indian Cuisine relies on basic multi-chain website portals and third-party delivery aggregators. Located at 1600 E Woodlawn Rd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its regional Indian culinary mastery, clay tandoor oven traditions, South Indian dosa craftsmanship, and Charlotte dining directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **SouthPark / Woodlawn Location:** 1600 E Woodlawn Rd, Ste 150, Charlotte NC 28209 (Woodlawn / Park Road / SouthPark Charlotte).
2. **Indian Culinary Specialties:** Crispy Golden Ghee Roast Dosas, Clay Tandoori Chicken & Kebabs, Hyderabadi Dum Biryani, Chettinad Spiced Curries, Fresh Tandoori Naan Breads, & South Indian Filter Coffee.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (980) 270-5888; Mon–Thu 11:30 AM – 2:15 PM & 5:30 PM – 9:45 PM | Fri 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM & 5:30 PM – 10:45 PM | Sat 11:30 AM – 10:45 PM | Sun 11:30 AM – 9:15 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Majestic Royal Display Typography:** Classical majestic display serif (*Marcellus*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and spice vault mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Royal Saffron Terracotta & Turmeric Amber Palette:** Traditional Indian palace palette anchored in deep royal terracotta (`#9A3412`), turmeric saffron amber (`#D97706`), deep emerald spice green (`#065F46`), and warm cardamom cream (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Dosa Craft & Tandoor Vault Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`south-indian-dosa-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing South Indian dosas and uthappams alongside North Indian clay-oven tandoori kebabs and dum biryanis.

### Inspected Recent Demos

### Divergent Choices for Nalan Indian Cuisine

- **Hero Composition:** Royal Indian Spice Palace & Tandoori Clay Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Indian Spice Crest Stamp badge (`"AUTHENTIC SOUTH & NORTH INDIAN ROYAL CUISINE • WOODLAWN CHARLOTTE"`), majestic display serif (*Marcellus*), deep royal terracotta & turmeric amber palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of golden crispy Mysore masala dosa.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Nalan Vault Cards** (`nalan-vault-card`) and Charlotte royal Indian dining highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`nalan-header`, `nalan-brand`, `spice-hero-stage`, `nalan-seal-badge`, `nalan-vault-card`, `nalan-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
