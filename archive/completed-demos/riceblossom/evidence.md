## Original Audit Weakness

Riceblossom relies on generic third-party online ordering portals (Grubhub/DoorDash) and basic web templates. As South Charlotte's premier authentic Szechuan dining destination on Park Road, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its Sichuan peppercorn heat profiles, high-flame wok hei technique, handmade dumpling craft, and Quail Corners takeout express directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South Charlotte Location:** 8418 Park Rd, Charlotte NC 28210 (Quail Corners / Park Road corridor).
2. **Szechuan & Chinese Culinary Offerings:** Chongqing Mala Spicy Chicken (La Zi Ji), Szechuan Boiled Fish & Beef in Chili Oil (Shuizhu), Silky Mapo Tofu with Fermented Doubanjiang, Handmade Pork Wontons in Red Chili Oil (Hong You Chao Shou), Sizzling Flaming Pan Specialties, Fried Rice & Noodle Wok Craft, and Artisanal Boba Fruit Teas.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 315-6989; Mon–Thu: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM & 4:30 PM – 9:30 PM | Fri: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM & 4:30 PM – 10:00 PM | Sat: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM | Sun: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Majestic Imperial Display Serif Typography:** Classical imperial display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Park Road kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Imperial Lacquer Red & Szechuan Peppercorn Gold Palette:** High-contrast Chengdu heat palette anchored in deep imperial lacquer red (`#881337`), chengdu ember dark (`#19080E`), szechuan peppercorn gold (`#F59E0B`), wok char amber (`#EA580C`), jasmine silk cream (`#FFF1F2`), and clean pure white.
3. **"The Chengdu Mala Spices & Sizzling Wok Hei Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`szechuan-wok-hei-and-mala-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing tongue-numbing peppercorn specialties, high-flame wok fried entrees, handmade chili wontons, and refreshing boba fruit teas.

- **Hero Composition:** Chengdu Fire & Szechuan Wok Chamber split-hero layout: left side features a Riceblossom Szechuan Seal badge (`"CHENGDU MALA HERITAGE • HIGH-HEAT WOK HEI • SOUTH CHARLOTTE"`), majestic display typography (*Cinzel*), imperial lacquer red & peppercorn gold palette, and right side features a framed hero image of steaming wok-fired Szechuan specialties with dried whole red chilies and Sichuan peppercorns.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Riceblossom Vault Cards** (`riceblossom-vault-card`) and Quail Corners wok kitchen highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`riceblossom-header`, `riceblossom-brand`, `szechuan-hero-stage`, `riceblossom-seal-badge`, `riceblossom-vault-card`, `riceblossom-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
