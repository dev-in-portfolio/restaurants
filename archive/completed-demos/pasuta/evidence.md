## Original Audit Weakness

Pasuta (often styled Pa Su Ta, the Japanese phonetic pronunciation of pasta / パスタ) is Charlotte’s singular destination for authentic *Wafu* pasta—the venerated Tokyo culinary tradition that unites Italian pasta craftsmanship with Japanese umami foundations. Operating inside the bustling Urban District Market food hall at 2315 North Davidson Street in the Mill District / NoDa neighborhood, Pasuta crafts house-extruded pasta noodles paired with innovative East-meets-West sauces: spicy mentaiko (cured cod roe) cream with shredded nori, sweet white miso mushroom butter with shimeji, black bean-infused bolognese, and perilla shiso pesto. Generic delivery scrapers frequently misclassify it as a generic Italian spaghetti stall or ramen kiosk, failing to highlight its specialized Wafu culinary philosophy, raw pasta retail provisions, or food hall hospitality.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Urban District Market Location:** 2315 N Davidson St, Charlotte, NC 28205 (Mill District / NoDa / Villa Heights food hall).
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Open Daily 11:00 AM – 10:30 PM (matching Urban District Market food hall hours).
3. **Signature Mentaiko Pasta:** Chewy al dente spaghetti tossed in a rich soy-butter and spicy cod roe (mentaiko) cream emulsion, finished with shredded toasted nori seaweed and shiso.
4. **Miso Mushroom Butter & Bacon Cream:** Fresh bucatini tossed with sauteed Japanese shimeji beech mushrooms, charred cabbage, scallions, and smoky bacon in a luscious white miso butter glaze.
5. **Fresh Handmade Pasta & Raw Take-Home Provisions:** Scratch-extruded pasta noodles (bucatini, spaghetti, tagliatelle, rigate) cooked fresh to order or packaged raw by the half-pound for home kitchens.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Wafu Nori & Miso Cream Palette:** Tokyo night obsidian (`#111315`), shiso slate (`#1c2022`), ceramic pasta bowl surface (`#272d30`), toasted nori amber (`#c98a3b`), shimeji cream gold (`#dfb15b`), and semolina flour white (`#f7f7f5`).
2. **Japanese-Italian Fusion Typography:** Refined modern high-contrast serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean legible modern body (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced pasta extrusion indexes (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A close-up plate of authentic Japanese Mentaiko spaghetti tossed in spicy roe cream with shredded nori; a modern flared bowl of creamy shimeji mushroom and bacon pasta; and fresh hand-cut pasta noodles dusted in flour on a wooden pasta-making bench.
