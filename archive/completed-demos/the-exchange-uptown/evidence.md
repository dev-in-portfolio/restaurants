## Original Audit Weakness

The Exchange Uptown is a boutique market, wine lounge, and gourmet grab-and-go destination located in the lobby of One Independence Center at 101 North Tryon Street (Unit L107), directly adjacent to Monarch Market in Uptown Charlotte. Serving corporate professionals, commuters, and center-city residents, the market offers a curated selection of wines by the glass ($7.00), rotating craft draft beers ($5.00), crisp mimosas ($6.00), fresh artisanal deli sandwiches, local North Carolina provisions (including Queen Charlotte Pimento Cheese), and specialty pantry goods. Despite its premier location, the venue relied on a minimal SpotHopper template without dedicated pages explaining its dual function as an afternoon wine lounge and morning/midday grab-and-go pantry, leaving catering clients and evening visitors without clear operational guidelines.
