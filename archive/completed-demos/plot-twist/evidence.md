## Original Audit Weakness

Plot Twist relies on basic social media updates and unbranded third-party directory listings. Located tucked away on Lyon Court in Plaza Midwood, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its cross-functional coffee/cocktail/zero-proof drink matrix, garage lounge aesthetics, food truck pop-up calendar, and Lyon Ct neighborhood parking directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Plaza Midwood Lyon Court Location:** 1518 Lyon Ct, Charlotte NC 28205.
2. **Cross-Functional Beverage Specialties:** Lavender Honey Espresso Lattes, Nitro Cold Brew, Espresso Martinis, Smoked Rosemary Old Fashioneds, Botanical Zero-Proof Elixirs, Craft Local Beers, and Weekend Food Truck Pop-Ups.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Tue–Thu 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM | Fri 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM | Sat 8:00 AM – 11:00 PM | Sun 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Mon Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **High-Contrast Fashion Serif Typography:** Expressive boutique display serif (*Prata*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Plaza Midwood garage ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Midnight Plum & Electric Magenta Palette:** Modern nightlife & botanical lounge palette anchored in deep midnight plum (`#1E1B4B`), velvet amethyst (`#2E1065`), electric magenta fuchsia (`#D946EF`), botanical citron lime (`#84CC16`), champagne blush (`#F5D0FE`), and pure white.
3. **"The Cross-Functional Beverage Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`cocktails-coffee-and-zeroproof-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing full-strength craft cocktails and espresso drinks alongside botanical zero-proof mocktails and weekend pop-up bites.

- **Hero Composition:** Midwood Garage Lounge & Botanical Cocktail Sanctuary split-hero layout: left side features a Midwood Third Space Seal badge (`"CONVERTED AUTO-GARAGE COFFEE & BOTANICAL COCKTAILS • PLAZA MIDWOOD"`), expressive boutique serif typography (*Prata*), midnight plum & electric magenta palette, and right side features a framed hero image of craft botanical cocktails with edible flower garnishes.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Twist Vault Cards** (`twist-vault-card`) and Plaza Midwood auto-garage highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`twist-header`, `twist-brand`, `garage-hero-stage`, `twist-seal-badge`, `twist-vault-card`, `twist-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
