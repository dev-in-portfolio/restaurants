Agave Heritage

“Mezcal is to Tequila what Scotch is to Whiskey.”

While Tequila is crafted exclusively from Blue Weber agave roasted in ovens, Mezcal represents a 500-year-old artisanal tradition where wild agaves mature up to 25 years before being roasted in underground pit ovens lined with volcanic rock.

At 1900’s Cantina, we serve every mezcal in traditional clay copitas or veladora glasses accompanied by fresh orange wheels and hand-ground Sal de Gusano (oaxacan agave salt).
