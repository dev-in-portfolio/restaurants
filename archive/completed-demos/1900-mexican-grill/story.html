Our Mexico City Heritage

A love letter to the neighborhood plazas, bustling street taquerias, and artisanal agave distillers of Mexico.

ESTABLISHED 2009 IN ELIZABETH

Born in Mexico City.
Stewarded in Charlotte.

When 1900 Mexican Grill opened its doors on Elizabeth Avenue in 2009, the mission was simple: break away from cookie-cutter Tex-Mex templates and recreate the soul of a true Mexico City neighborhood cantina.

The name "1900" pays homage to a turning point in Mexican culinary history — when traditional regional recipes converged in the grand plazas of Mexico City, giving birth to modern street taquería culture.

Over 16 years, 1900 has expanded to South Park on Park Road while preserving the exact scratch kitchen techniques that earned Charlotte's loyalty.

The Three Pillars of 1900

24-Ingredient Scratch Mole

Our signature Mole Poblano is slow-roasted over 8 hours using dried mulato, ancho, and pasilla chiles, Mexican dark chocolate, plantains, almonds, and warm spices. No store-bought pastes, ever.

🏺

Barrels Selected at the Source

Every year, our team travels to small distilleries in Jalisco and Oaxaca to sample directly from oak barrels, selecting custom Private Reserve Tequilas exclusively bottled for 1900 guests.
