## Original Audit Weakness

Saffron Indian Cuisine is a cornerstone dining destination in Southwest Charlotte's vibrant Ayrsley Town Center. However, its online presence is fragmented across third-party delivery services with confusing overlap with an unrelated restaurant in Cary, NC. It lacked an owned, elegant digital flagship that articulates its tandoor clay-oven craft, saffron-infused royal heritage recipes, extensive vegetarian/vegan options, and lunch/dinner hospitality.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Ayrsley Town Center Location:** 2135 Ayrsley Town Boulevard, Suite B, Charlotte NC 28273. Anchoring the pedestrian-friendly Ayrsley Town entertainment and business district near I-485.
2. **Traditional Indian Culinary Program:** Tandoori clay oven roasted meats (Tandoori Chicken, Malai Tikka), fragrant curries (Chicken Tikka Masala, Lamb Rogan Josh, Paneer Tikka Masala, Channa Masala), fresh tandoor breads (Saffron Butter Naan, Garlic Naan, Roti), Vegetable Biryani, crispy samosas, and chilled mango lassi.
3. **Contact & Hours:** Phone (980) 297-7722; Open Daily: Lunch 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM, Dinner 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM. Dine-in, takeout, and corporate catering.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Royal Saffron & Kashmiri Ruby Palette:** Regal Indian warmth rooted in royal midnight obsidian (`#0d090a`), deep garnet card (`#1a1013`), warm saffron gold (`#eab308`), Kashmiri saffron crimson (`#b91c1c`), warm turmeric amber (`#f59e0b`), and rich ivory linen (`#fefce8`).
2. **Refined Serif Typography:** Regal literary serif (*Cinzel* or *Marcellus* paired with *Plus Jakarta Sans*) with high-contrast elegance, accompanied by traditional spice-route metadata accents.
3. **Authentic Indian Photography:** Vibrant yellow saffron basmati rice with whole saffron threads paired with rich Chicken Tikka Masala, blistered clay-oven tandoori chicken drumsticks with mint raita, and hot charred garlic naan glistening in an Indian basket.
