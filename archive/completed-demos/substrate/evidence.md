## Original Audit Weakness

Substrate is an acclaimed natural wine, artisanal vermouth, and amaro bar located at 512 East 15th Street in Charlotte's Optimist Park / Belmont neighborhood. Self-described as a "blue-collar enoteca," it pairs low-intervention wines, Spanish vermouth on tap, highbrow-lowbrow cocktail classics (like the "Spaghett" High Life with Aperol), and tinned seafood conservas with an all-analog vinyl record sound system and open patio. Standard directory scrapers often mischaracterize it as either a generic night club or a full-service sit-down restaurant, completely missing its distinct enoteca identity, vinyl turntable focus, and bring-your-own-food hospitality model.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Optimist Park Location:** 512 East 15th Street, Charlotte, NC 28205.
2. **Weekly Operating Hours:** Monday: 4:00 PM – 10:00 PM; Tuesday: Closed; Wednesday – Thursday: 4:00 PM – 10:00 PM; Friday: 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM; Saturday: 12:00 PM – 12:00 AM; Sunday: 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM.
3. **Natural Wine & Vermouth on Tap:** Unfiltered skin-contact orange wines, pet-nats, chilled glou-glou reds, and draft Spanish vermut served over ice with Gordal olives and orange wheels.
4. **Analog Vinyl Hi-Fi Program:** Deep vinyl record collection and analog turntables spinning soul, jazz, post-punk, and vintage funk.
5. **Conservas & Blue-Collar Enoteca Snacks:** Curated Spanish tinned sardines, smoked mussels, artisan cured charcuterie, pickled peppers, and buttered saltines, alongside an open BYOF (bring your own food) welcoming policy.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Optimist Park Industrial Rust & Slate Palette:** Deep vinyl obsidian (`#11100f`), warm cellar slate (`#1c1a18`), rusted industrial terracotta (`#b8502d`), vermouth amber (`#df8f3b`), and vintage record paper (`#f5f2eb`).
2. **Analog Editorial Typography:** Industrial serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean utilitarian sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced vinyl catalog tags (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Natural Wine & Vinyl Photography:** Stemmed wine glass on outdoor patio table at twilight, artisan charcuterie & tapas snack board, and hands cueing up an analog vinyl LP on a wooden turntable.
