## Original Audit Weakness

Oh My Soul relies on basic third-party online ordering portals and basic web pages. Located at 3046 N Davidson St in NoDa, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its 100% vegan South African culinary heritage, Durban curry specialties, historic oak patio sanctuary atmosphere, and NoDa gathering directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **NoDa Arts District Charlotte Location:** 3046 N Davidson St, Charlotte NC 28205 (NoDa Arts District).
2. **Plant-Based South African Specialties:** Durban "Bunny Chow" (spiced chickpea curry in a hollowed sourdough loaf), Loaded "V-Dog" craft sausages, Signature Big "V" Beyond Burgers, V-Licious Waffles, South African crunchies, botanical cocktails, and 100-year-old oak patio dining.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 891-4664; Wed–Fri 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Sat 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Sun 10:00 AM – 4:30 PM | Mon–Tue Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Afro-Bohemian Geometric Sans Display Typography:** Warm contemporary sans (*Outfit*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and safari field journal mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Jungle Forest & Sunburst Marigold Palette:** Lush botanical sanctuary palette anchored in deep jungle forest green (`#064E3B`), sunburst marigold yellow (`#F59E0B`), protea coral red (`#F43F5E`), and lush mint leaf cream (`#F0FDF4`).
3. **"The South African Comfort Fare & Botanical Drinks Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`south-african-comfort-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing Durban curry loaves alongside giant craft vegan burgers and botanical cocktails.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Oh My Ganache, The Cheesecake Bar` — Modern Artisan Confectionery with Playfair Display fonts and Dark Cocoa/Raspberry Rose.
2. `Oaklore Bar & Bottle Shop` — Charred Oak & Copper Distillery with DM Serif Display fonts and Charred Oak/Copper.
3. `Nora Mac’s Traditional Irish Pub` — Dublin Celtic Public House with Cinzel fonts and Stout Onyx/Shamrock Emerald.

### Divergent Choices for Oh My Soul

- **Hero Composition:** South African Botanical Garden Sanctuary & Vibrant Plant-Based Kitchen split-hero layout: left side features an African Sun Stamp badge (`"100% PLANT-BASED SOUTH AFRICAN SANCTUARY • NODA CHARLOTTE"`), vibrant Afro-boho sans-serif display typography (*Outfit*), deep jungle forest green & sunburst marigold palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of shaded patio dining under the historic 100-year-old oak tree.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Soul Sanctuary Cards** (`soul-vault-card`) and Charlotte NoDa plant-based community highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`soul-header`, `soul-brand`, `sanctuary-hero-stage`, `soul-seal-badge`, `soul-vault-card`, `soul-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
