## Original Audit Weakness

Yummm Donuts is a premier independent artisan bakery located in South Charlotte's Tyvola Square (624 Tyvola Road, Suite 104, Charlotte, NC 28217). Renowned for fresh-fried, cloud-soft yeast donuts prepared daily at 5:00 AM, the bakery offers over 20 artisanal varieties including Maple Bacon, Cookies & Cream, S'mores, Red Velvet, Funfetti Sprinkles, giant glazed apple fritters, 12-piece Yummm Bites donut holes, and cold-brew iced coffees. Despite having an intensely loyal morning following, the business lacked a standalone, independent web presence—relying entirely on fragmented third-party delivery listings (DoorDash, Grubhub, Uber Eats) with heavy commission fees, missing direct bakery pre-order mechanisms for corporate dozens and school events, and failing to showcase its scratch-made baking craft.
