## Original Audit Weakness

The Story of Mi Cariño is South End Charlotte’s acclaimed chef-driven gastro-cantina and mezcalería, located at 1440 South Tryon Street, Suite 100. Centered on "Left Coast Latin" culinary philosophy, Mi Cariño fuses traditional coastal Mexican culinary roots with California produce-driven creativity, featuring seared Akaushi Wagyu ribeye, Truffle Street Corn Dip, Ceviche Tropicoso, and slow-braised Pork Belly Pibil. Furthermore, the establishment houses an unrivaled beverage collection of over 200 agave-based spirits, including artisanal mezcals, rare raicillas, and estate tequilas. Despite near-universal culinary acclaim and packed weekend services, third-party aggregators often categorize Mi Cariño as a standard neighborhood taco bar, failing to convey its Wagyu steak program, ceviche craft, high-end mezcal library, cashless policy, and reservation etiquette.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Location:** 1440 S Tryon St, Suite 100, Charlotte, NC 28203.
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Tuesday – Wednesday 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM; Thursday – Saturday 5:00 PM – 12:00 AM; Closed Sunday & Monday.
3. **Left Coast Latin Culinary Heritage:** Akaushi Wagyu Ribeye with charred scallion chimichurri, Ceviche Tropicoso with citrus leche de tigre and avocado, Truffle Street Corn Dip, Camarones Kora, and Popcorn Flan.
4. **200+ Agave & Mezcal Program:** Handcrafted Oaxaca Old Fashioneds, smoked grapefruit Palomas in crystal highballs, and curated single-village mezcal flights.
5. **Atmosphere & Etiquette:** Cashless policy, intimate candlelit dining room, and advanced table reservations via Resy.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Agave Terracotta & Cantina Palette:** Oaxacan midnight (`#0e0f12`), charred agave charcoal (`#18191e`), weathered terracotta (`#c66543`), warm copita gold (`#dfa857`), desert cactus green (`#3d6756`), and sea salt linen (`#f7f6f2`).
2. **Left Coast Latin Typography:** Bold architectural serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean contemporary sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and cantina agave monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A thick prime Akaushi Wagyu ribeye steak seared with roasted garlic cloves; a vibrant plate of citrus-cured seafood ceviche with sliced cucumbers, red onion, and avocado on a crisp corn tostada; and a handcrafted crystal highball Paloma cocktail garnished with fresh lime and orange wedges.
