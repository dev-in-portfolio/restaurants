## Original Audit Weakness

Ninety’s Dessert Bar relies on basic website templates and third-party delivery aggregators. Located at 9009 J M Keynes Dr on the University Boardwalk, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its immersive 90s pop-culture theme, extreme milkshake engineering, custom party catering, and Charlotte dessert directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **University City Boardwalk Location:** 9009 J M Keynes Dr #4, Charlotte NC 28262 (Boardwalk at University Place / University City Charlotte).
2. **Nostalgic 90s Dessert Specialties:** Extreme Handcrafted Freakshakes (Campfire S'mores, Dunkaroo Frosting Dip, Fruity Pebbles Cereal Infusions), Warm Stuffed Cookie Skillets, Giant Macaron Ice Cream Sandwiches, Artisan Scoops, & Specialty Cheesecakes.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 547-1856; Sun–Thu 12:00 PM – 9:30 PM | Fri–Sat 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Funky Retro 90s Display Typography:** Radical retro display font (*Righteous*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and arcade high-score mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Memphis Electric Teal & Neon Pink Palette:** High-energy 90s pop aesthetic anchored in electric teal cyan (`#06B6D4`), hot neon pink (`#F43F5E`), laser arcade yellow (`#FACC15`), and arcade slate navy (`#0F172A`).
3. **"The Extreme Freakshake & Cookie Skillet Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`extreme-freakshakes-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing towering cereal milkshakes alongside warm stuffed cookie skillets and macaron sandwiches.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Niki’s Food Shop` — Vintage Charlotte Southern Diner with Alfa Slab One fonts and Hickory Brown/Biscuit Gold.
2. `Night Swim Coffee` — Modern Nordic Minimalist Roastery with Epilogue fonts and Ocean Navy/Wave Cyan.
3. `Nalan Indian Cuisine` — Royal Indian Spice Palace with Marcellus fonts and Royal Terracotta/Turmeric Amber.

### Divergent Choices for Ninety’s Dessert Bar

- **Hero Composition:** Radical 90s Pop Retro Memphis & Neon Milkshake Vault split-hero layout: left side features a 90s Nostalgia Stamp badge (`"RADICAL 90S EXTREME MILKSHAKE & DESSERT BAR • UNIVERSITY CHARLOTTE"`), ultra-bold funky retro display typography (*Righteous*), electric teal & hot neon pink palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of signature layered cereal ice cream treats.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Ninety's Vault Cards** (`ninetys-vault-card`) and Charlotte University Boardwalk dessert highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`ninetys-header`, `ninetys-brand`, `arcade-hero-stage`, `ninetys-seal-badge`, `ninetys-vault-card`, `ninetys-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
