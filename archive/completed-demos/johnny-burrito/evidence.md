## Original Audit Weakness

Johnny Burrito is one of Uptown Charlotte's most legendary weekday lunch destinations, hidden in the subterranean plaza of Two Wells Fargo Center. Despite cult-status popularity and lines stretching through the atrium, their digital footprint was limited to basic third-party review listings without an interactive burrito assembly guide, live tamale day schedules, or corporate catering order sheets. This 6-page concept provides an energetic, modern digital home matching their lively lunch counter.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Big California Burritos:** 13-inch flavored gourmet tortillas (Jalapeño Cheddar, Spinach Herb, Chipotle, Flour) rolled with seasoned steak, grilled chicken, slow-cooked carnitas, cilantro-lime rice, black beans, Monterey jack cheese, guacamole, and roasted salsas.
2. **Tuesday & Friday Tamale Ritual:** Authentic scratch masa steamed in corn husks with seasoned shredded pork or chicken and red enchilada gravy (selling out quickly every week).
3. **The Rookie Card Culture:** Welcoming first-time diners with the iconic "I'm a Rookie!" callout, handing them a custom rookie card, and guiding them down the lightning-fast assembly line.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Mission Gold & Cilantro Green Palette:** Sunburst Mission Gold (`#f59e0b`), Avocado Cilantro Green (`#15803d`), Roasted Chili Crimson (`#dc2626`), and Warm Masa Cream (`#fdfbf7`).
2. **Burrito Bar Layout Architecture:** 13-inch tortilla flavor matrices, salsa spice scale callouts, and tamale day countdown banners.
3. **Energetic High-Speed Typography:** Syne bold display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for rapid lunch customization.
