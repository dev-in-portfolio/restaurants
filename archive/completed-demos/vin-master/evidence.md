## Original Audit Weakness

Vin Master is historic South End's beloved boutique natural wine bar and bottle shop located inside the Atherton Mill complex at 2000 South Blvd. Famous for its approachable low-intervention and biodynamic wines, flight paddles organized by flavor profile, warm flatbreads, and direct Rail Trail outdoor patio access, its original web presence lacked responsive multi-page depth, comprehensive tasting schedules, and immersive natural wine education.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Natural Terroir & Cellar Palette:** Dark Vine Charcoal (`#151416`), Cellar Oak Clay (`#1f1c20`), Tasting Panel (`#2a252d`), Natural Terracotta Amber (`#c87d55`), Olive Leaf Sage (`#6b8e23`), and Warm Linen Cream (`#faf7f2`).
2. **Refined Classical Serif & Modern Sans Typography:** Display serif (*Cinzel* / *Playfair Display*) paired with clean geometric body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and sommelier cellar ticket monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Floating Hero Badge:** Concrete operational facts (`Vin Master | Atherton Mill • South End Charlotte NC`).
4. **Editorial Layout:** Three-card standards grid without emojis, followed by dual alternating highlight banners (`.vin-highlight-banner`, `.reverse`).
