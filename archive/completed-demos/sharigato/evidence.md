## Original Audit Weakness

Sharigato is Charlotte’s pioneer dedicated Japanese hand-roll (temaki) bar, located at 420 East 22nd Street in the Area 15 / Optimist Park corridor. Rooted in the Japanese philosophy of serving hand rolls one at a time over a central wooden counter to ensure maximum nori crispness and warm shari rice contrast, third-party dining directories frequently mislabel Sharigato as an ordinary takeout sushi joint or generic roll parlor without highlighting their intimate counter omakase-style pacing, Ariake Sea seaweed standards, or specific hand-roll set menus.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Optimist Park / Area 15 Location:** Located at 420 E 22nd St, Charlotte NC 28206. Dedicated Japanese hand-roll bar counter. Email: `hello@sharigato.com`.
2. **The "Shari + Arigato" Heritage:** Name honors "Shari" (the soul of sushi—warm, seasoned sushi rice) and "Arigato" (gratitude).
3. **One-By-One Counter Service:** Hand rolls are passed directly from chef to diner one at a time to be eaten within seconds before the toasted nori softens from the warm rice.
4. **Structured Hand-Roll Sets:** 3-Roll, 4-Roll, 5-Roll, and 6-Roll curated sets featuring Bluefin Toro, King Salmon, Yellowtail Scallion, Maine Lobster, and Spicy Scallop.
5. **Operating Schedule:** Tue–Sat: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Last seating at 7:00 PM); Sun: 12:00 PM – 7:00 PM; Mon: Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Wabi-Sabi Charcoal & Hinoki Cedar Palette:** Minimalist charcoal black (`#0f1011`), hinoki cedar wood card (`#1a1917`), matcha green accent (`#4a7c59`), gold foil (`#d4af37`), and wasabi cream (`#f7f5f0`).
2. **Zen Architectural Editorial Typography:** Refined serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced rice/timing specifications (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Temaki & Artisan Sashimi Photography:** Spicy tuna temaki hand roll presented in hand at the counter, salmon roe (ikura) temaki on ceramic plate, and masterfully sliced sashimi crudo.

- **HTML Vocabulary:** Bespoke classes (`sharigato-header`, `sharigato-brand`, `sharigato-hero-stage`, `sharigato-hero-badge`, `sharigato-standards-section`, `sharigato-standard-card`, `sharigato-highlight-banner`, `sharigato-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
