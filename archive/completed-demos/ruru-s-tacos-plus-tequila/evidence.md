## Original Audit Weakness

RuRu’s Tacos + Tequila occupies one of Charlotte’s most striking and storied historic architectural properties—the historic Reynolds-Gourmajenko Tuscan villa at 715 Providence Road in Myers Park, featuring an expansive courtyard terrace and stone fountain. However, generic online representations fail to highlight the restaurant’s playful pop-culture menu architecture, its 100+ bottle agave tequila library, its signature garden patio ambiance, and its fresh scratch Mexican cooking.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Historic Myers Park Tuscan Villa & Garden Fountain Patio:** Situated at 715 Providence Rd, Charlotte NC 28207 in historic Eastover / Myers Park. Famous for its sweeping outdoor courtyard terrace centered around a stone fountain.
2. **Iconic Pop-Culture & Movie-Named Tacos:** Repertoire featuring legendary names: "I’ll Have What She’s Having" (crispy chicken), "You Got Knocked The F*** Out" (marinated grilled steak), "Fat Guy in a Little Coat" (slow-braised carnitas), "Magic Mike" (charred mahi-mahi), and "Stage Five Clinger" (crispy shrimp).
3. **Agave Bar & Scratch Mixology:** Curated library of 100+ blanco, reposado, añejo, and mezcal spirits, house-squeezed lime margaritas, and fresh palomas.
4. **Hours & Walk-In Policy:** Sun: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM, Mon–Thu: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM, Fri–Sat: 11:00 AM – 9:30 PM. Walk-in seating only (no reservations). Direct phone: (704) 946-8161.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Cantina Agave Gold & Sun-Drenched Villa Terracotta Palette:** Cantina obsidian night (`#120e0b`), warm terracotta clay card (`#1c1511`), agave gold primary accent (`#e5a93b`), crisp habanero crimson (`#d64527`), and sunlit lime cream (`#fdfbf7`).
2. **Bespoke Historic Villa Typography:** Distinctive rustic serif (*Cinzel*) paired with contemporary geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced price counters (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Cantina Photography:** Trio of street tacos with fresh cilantro and lime wedges on a white plate over warm wood, classic coupe margarita with fine sea-salt rim, and fresh avocado guacamole with crispy tortilla chips.
