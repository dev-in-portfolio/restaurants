## Original Audit Weakness

The Public House is an elevated Uptown Charlotte tavern, craft cocktail lounge, and sports gathering hub located at 300 S Brevard St, Suite B100, positioned strategically between the Charlotte Convention Center, Spectrum Center, and Bank of America Stadium. Renowned for its late-night kitchen, prime cut Steak N' Frites with garlic truffle butter, double-patty "Remedy Burger" with bacon onion jam, Asian loaded tots, and craft bourbon cocktails, The Public House bridges upscale gastropub dining and energetic game-day hospitality. Despite high local demand among Uptown professionals and sports fans, third-party delivery apps often mislabel daytime hours because the venue opens at 4:00 PM on weekdays, and scrape generic text that completely misses its signature scratch culinary program, convention catering capabilities, and curated bourbon library.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Uptown Location:** 300 S Brevard St, Suite B100, Charlotte, NC 28202 (at 300 South Brevard, steps from LYNX 3rd St / Convention Center station).
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Monday – Thursday 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM; Friday 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Saturday 11:00 AM – 2:00 AM; Sunday 11:00 AM – 12:00 AM. Happy Hour Monday – Friday 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM.
3. **Elevated Tavern Fare:** The Remedy Burger (double custom beef patties, sharp cheddar, bacon jam, brioche bun), Steak N' Frites (prime seared flat iron steak, herb compound butter, parmesan frites, demi-glace), and Bangin' Crispy Shrimp.
4. **Late-Night & Game-Day Shareables:** Asian loaded tots drizzled with unagi glaze and sriracha aioli, truffle parmesan frites, giant salted warm Bavarian pretzels with house beer cheese, and crispy buttermilk wings.
5. **Craft Cocktails & Bourbon:** Hand-cut crystal ice sphere Old Fashioneds, smoked bourbons, espresso martinis, and Carolina craft drafts on tap.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Uptown Tavern Palette:** Midnight obsidian (`#101216`), tavern slate (`#1a1f26`), warm walnut wood (`#272420`), amber bourbon gold (`#d4883b`), bourbon hover (`#b56e29`), crisp tavern linen (`#f4f3ef`), and crimson neon accent (`#c94a38`).
2. **Gastropub & Architectural Typography:** Modern architectural display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean editorial sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and transit-line monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A sizzling double bacon cheeseburger with melted sharp cheddar and crispy french fries on a pub platter; prime seared Steak N' Frites topped with melting herb compound butter and golden frites; and a handcrafted whiskey Old Fashioned served over a crystal clear ice sphere with an orange twist.
