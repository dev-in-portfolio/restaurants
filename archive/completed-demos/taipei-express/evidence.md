## Original Audit Weakness

Taipei Express is one of Charlotte’s longest-running and most beloved Chinese and Taiwanese wok kitchens, located at 731 Providence Road in the historic Eastover neighborhood. Operating for decades as an essential neighborhood fixture, Taipei Express is celebrated for its high-heat wok hei searing, made-to-order General Tso’s chicken, pan-fried pork dumplings, and hand-tossed egg lo mein. Third-party aggregator scrapers frequently misrepresent it as a generic fast-food chain or strip-mall counter, ignoring its neighborhood legacy, scratch dumpling preparation, family-run continuity, and loyal multi-generational customer base.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Historic Eastover Location:** 731 Providence Rd, Charlotte, NC 28207 (corner of Providence Road).
2. **Weekly Operating Hours:** Mon–Fri 11:00 AM – 9:30 PM, Sat–Sun 12:00 PM – 9:30 PM (Lunch specials served daily until 3:00 PM).
3. **High-Heat Wok Hei Cooking:** Dishes seared in roaring steel woks at 600°F+, locking in tenderness, crisp vegetable texture, and caramelized aromatics.
4. **House Specialties:** Hand-pleated pan-fried pork potstickers with seasoned ginger soy, General Tso’s chicken with steamed broccoli, Crispy Sesame chicken, Wok Beef with Broccoli, and golden Crab Rangoon.
5. **Noodles & Soups:** Hand-tossed egg lo mein with scallions, house fried rice, and comforting scratch Hot & Sour and Wonton soups.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Providence Wok Crimson & Jade Palette:** Imperial wok lacquer (`#171214`), porcelain slate (`#251d20`), fiery wok crimson (`#c7382c`), imperial gold (`#d89b34`), and jasmine rice white (`#faf7f2`).
2. **Heritage Eastover Asian Typography:** Classic heritage display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean utilitarian sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced kitchen station callouts (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** Wok-glazed chicken morsels with crisp broccoli and carrots over jasmine rice; pan-fried golden pork potstickers on blue-and-white porcelain; and wok-tossed golden egg lo mein noodles.
