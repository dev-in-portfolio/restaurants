## Original Audit Weakness

VINYL is an unpretentious music-centric neighborhood bar, craft cocktail lounge, and elevated street food kitchen located at 1440 South Tryon Street (Suite 105) in Charlotte's South End. Built as an homage to the golden eras of vinyl records, the lounge features an expansive wrap-around bar, Charlotte's only operational mechanical split flap board, nightly resident DJs, and a founding USBG (United States Bartenders’ Guild) Guildhouse pedigree. Its culinary program—led by Culinary Director Vince Giancarlo and Chef de Cuisine Jacob Hujer—serves scratch-made elevated bar classics such as the signature double ribeye & brisket VINYL Burger ($17), peri peri chicken sandwiches ($15), garlic bread pizzas on local Verdant Bread focaccia, and inventive craft cocktails by Beverage Director Henry Schmulling. Despite its critical acclaim across Eater Carolinas, Axios, and the Charlotte Observer, VINYL relied on a slow single-page Webflow design with nested accordion modals that failed to provide dedicated pages for its signature cocktail collections, culinary street food, and private music event buyouts.
