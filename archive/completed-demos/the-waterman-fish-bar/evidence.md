## Original Audit Weakness

The Waterman Fish Bar is South End’s premier neighborhood oyster house and seafood bar, located at 2729 South Boulevard in Charlotte. Renowned for its lively raw bar shucking fresh Carolina, Virginia, and Gulf Coast oysters over crushed ice, along with its famous "$1.50 Oyster Happy Hour" (Monday through Friday from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM), The Waterman brings coastal seafood shack authenticity into an energetic urban environment. Crowning the building is "Topside," its second-story open-air rooftop bar featuring skyline sunset views, frozen cocktails, and coastal craft drafts. Despite widespread regional acclaim for its scratch Maine lobster rolls, beer-battered fish and chips, and chargrilled garlic butter oysters, third-party delivery scrapers reduce The Waterman to a generic fast-casual fried fish listing, entirely omitting its dedicated raw bar provenance, Topside rooftop experience, daily shucking ritual, and coastal cocktail program.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Location:** 2729 South Blvd, Suite D, Charlotte, NC 28209.
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Sunday – Thursday 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM; Friday – Saturday 11:00 AM – 11:00 PM. Topside Rooftop Bar open Wednesday – Friday from 2:00 PM to close, Saturday – Sunday from 11:00 AM to close.
3. **Fresh Raw Bar & $1.50 Happy Hour:** Hand-shucked Carolina and boutique Atlantic oysters on beds of crushed ice with house shallot mignonette, cocktail sauce, grated horseradish, and lemon wedges; $1.50 house oysters weekdays 2–7 PM.
4. **Coastal Specialties:** Maine Lobster Roll with tender knuckle and claw meat on toasted split brioche buns; beer-battered Atlantic whitefish & chips; spicy Voodoo shrimp; and chargrilled oysters bubbling with parmesan and garlic herb butter.
5. **Topside Rooftop Lounge:** Second-story open-air deck overlooking South End with frozen tropical drinks, Aperol spritzes, and cold Carolina IPAs.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Maritime Coastal & Shucked Oyster Palette:** Deep nautical navy (`#0f1c2b`), oyster slate (`#1c2d3d`), coastal driftwood (`#2b3f52`), seafoam sage (`#4ea699`), oyster pearl cream (`#f6f8fa`), and sunset coral amber (`#e2725b`).
2. **Coastal Oyster Bar & Maritime Typography:** Refined maritime display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean nautical sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and tide-chart coordinate monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A platter of fresh raw oysters on the half shell over crushed ice with lemon slices and parsley; an authentic Maine lobster roll on a griddled split bun served with sea salt kettle chips on a wooden bar table; and golden beer-battered fish and chips with hand-cut fries and tartar sauce.
