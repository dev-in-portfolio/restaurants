## Original Audit Weakness

Thai House is Charlotte’s foundational Thai culinary institution, first established in 1995 in University City and now serving the community through its Cotswold flagship at 110 South Sharon Amity Road and its University location at 230 East W.T. Harris Boulevard. Celebrated for over three decades for its authentic Royal Thai recipes—creamy Green and Panang curries scented with kaffir lime and Thai holy basil, wok-seared Pad Thai with jumbo shrimp, crispy Chicken Satay with house peanut sauce, and aromatic Tom Yum soup—Thai House has introduced generations of Charlotteans to Thai gastronomy. Generic food aggregators frequently flatten Thai House into a basic Asian takeout listing, obscuring its 31-year heritage, formal Southeast Asian hospitality, and scratch coconut-milk curry craftsmanship.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Cotswold & University Locations:** 110 S Sharon Amity Rd, Charlotte, NC 28211 (Cotswold flagship) & 230 E W.T. Harris Blvd B9, Charlotte, NC 28262 (University City).
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule (Cotswold):** Mon–Thu 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM, Fri 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM, Sat 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM, Sun 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM.
3. **Wok-Tossed Pad Thai:** Traditional stir-fried rice noodles tossed in tamarind sauce with plump jumbo shrimp, scrambled egg, bean sprouts, scallions, and crushed roasted peanuts.
4. **Royal Coconut Milk Curries:** Slow-simmered Thai Green Curry with bamboo shoots and Thai basil, creamy Panang Curry with kaffir lime leaves, and rich peanut Masaman curry served with steamed jasmine rice.
5. **Scratch Street Appetizers & Soups:** Flame-grilled Chicken Satay skewers served over banana leaf with rich spiced peanut sauce; hot and sour Tom Yum Goong; and silky coconut milk Tom Kha Gai.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Teakwood Emerald & Golden Saffron Palette:** Deep teakwood obsidian (`#101512`), bamboo jade slate (`#1c2520`), royal Thai silk surface (`#26332c`), golden lotus saffron (`#d49e35`), Thai lemongrass green (`#4e8a5b`), and jasmine rice white (`#f8faf7`).
2. **Royal Southeast Asian Typography:** Elegant display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean legible modern body (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced kitchen spice indexes (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A fresh plate of savory shrimp Pad Thai noodles with crushed peanuts; an authentic bowl of Thai Green Curry with coconut milk, red chili, and a dome of steamed jasmine rice; and flame-grilled Chicken Satay skewers coated in warm peanut sauce.
