## Original Audit Weakness

The Lodge: A Sportsman’s Grill is South Charlotte’s premier rustic sports tavern and grill, located at 7725 Colony Road inside the Colony Place shopping center. Modeled after an authentic Blue Ridge mountain hunting lodge with rough-hewn timber beams, stone accents, sportsman taxidermy, and over 25 high-definition game screens, The Lodge sets itself apart from cookie-cutter corporate chains through an ambitious scratch kitchen. Known across Mecklenburg County for its fresh-ground Carolina Bison and Angus burgers, handmade bacon-wrapped jalapeno poppers stuffed with sharp pimento cheese, slow-smoked BBQ pork ribs, and blackened fish tacos, third-party delivery scrapers often reduce it to a generic burger shack, failing to present its rustic lodge aesthetic, craft taproom selection, daily specials (like Meatloaf Monday), or game-day viewing hospitality.

2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Sunday 10:00 AM – 12:00 AM; Monday – Saturday 11:00 AM – 12:00 AM.
3. **Carolina Bison & Angus Burgers:** Hand-pattied beef and lean game bison seared on the flat top, topped with melted aged cheddar, crisp applewood bacon, house burger sauce, and seasoned fries.
4. **Slow-Smoked BBQ Ribs & Wings:** Tender hickory-smoked pork ribs glazed in sweet tangy barbecue sauce with coleslaw and french fries, alongside crispy jumbo chicken wings tossed in dry rub, classic buffalo, or honey habanero.
5. **Handmade Sportsman Starters:** Bacon-wrapped fresh jalapenos stuffed with homemade southern pimento cheese; seared ahi tuna with ponzu; and loaded potato skins with sour cream and chives.

1. **Blue Ridge Timber & Hearth Charcoal Palette:** Timber bark obsidian (`#151210`), hearth charcoal (`#231e1a`), rough-hewn cedar (`#312821`), timber antler gold (`#c88d3e`), amber flame (`#dd7a32`), and mountain snow cream (`#f6f4f0`).
2. **Sportsman Lodge & Tavern Typography:** Stately rugged display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean legible modern body (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced tavern/draft indexes (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A thick seared cheeseburger with melted cheddar and golden fries served on a rustic carved wooden board; a slab of glistening barbecue pork ribs with french fries, coleslaw, and a tall frosted glass of beer; and a platter of crispy golden buffalo wings with bleu cheese dressing and celery.
