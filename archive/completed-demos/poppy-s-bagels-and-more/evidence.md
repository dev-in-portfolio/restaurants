## Original Audit Weakness

Poppy’s Bagels & More relies on basic third-party online ordering plugins and fragmented multi-location directory links. With two prime Charlotte locations on Providence Rd and South Blvd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its kettle-boiling and hearth-baking heritage, custom breakfast sandwich builder, Jewish deli carving board, office catering platter engines, and multi-location pickup directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Dual Charlotte Locations:**
   - Providence Flagship: 2921 Providence Rd, Charlotte NC 28211 (Providence / Cotswold) — Phone: (704) 366-8146.
   - South End Location: 2201 South Blvd, Charlotte NC 28203 (South End) — Phone: (704) 900-5107.
2. **NYC Kettle & Deli Specialties:** Kettle-Boiled Everything Bagels, Nova Scotia Lox Schmears, Taylor Ham Pork Roll Egg & Cheese, Hot NY Pastrami on Rye, Hot Corned Beef Reubens, Smoked Whitefish Salad, and Whipped House Cream Cheeses.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Both locations open 7 days a week: Mon–Sun 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM Daily.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Heavy NYC Vintage Deli Serif Typography:** Bold heritage serif (*Fraunces*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Brooklyn deli ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Brooklyn Navy & Deli Mustard Palette:** Classic New York appetizing palette anchored in deep Brooklyn navy (`#0F172A`), toasted poppyseed slate (`#1E293B`), NYC deli mustard (`#EAB308`), pastrami crimson (`#DC2626`), warm dough cream (`#FEF3C7`), and clean linen white.
3. **"The Kettle-Boiled Bagel & Hot Deli Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`kettle-bagels-and-deli-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing kettle-boiled bagels and hot deli sandwiches alongside breakfast wraps and whipped cream cheese spreads.
