## Original Audit Weakness

El Rinconcito Chilango is revered by food critics and East Charlotte locals as one of the most authentic Mexico City street taquerias in North Carolina, famous for hand-pressed tortillas and simmering copper cazo suadero. However, because it is located inside Rodriguez Supermarket, it lacks an official owned website, forcing diners to rely on informal word-of-mouth or third-party delivery listings with missing dish stories, salsa heat ratings, and inside-market pickup directions. This 6-page concept provides an authoritative, bilingual digital home honoring their CDMX street craft.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Copper Cazo & Trompo Street Meats:** Authentic suadero (slow-simmered beef rose meat in copper cazo), rotisserie al pastor with pineapple, crispy tripa dorada, and Campechanos (suadero + longaniza + chicharrón).
2. **Hand-Pressed Masa & Giant Tortas:** Scratch corn masa pressed to order on hot comales, giant Torta Cubana with breaded milanesa, and guajillo-drenched Pambazos stuffed with potatoes and chorizo.
3. **Specialty Regional Ingredients:** Fresh Flor de Calabaza, Huitlacoche, imported Queso Oaxaca, and house-made roasted chile de árbol salsa.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Chipotle Crimson & Aztec Sun Palette:** Chipotle Crimson (`#9e2a2b`), Aztec Sun Gold (`#e76f51`), Cilantro Leaf Green (`#2a9d8f`), and Tortilla Cream (`#faf6ed`).
2. **Street Taqueria Visual Architecture:** Sizzling cazo callouts, salsa heat indicators, and hand-pressed masa step diagrams.
3. **Bold Energetic Typography:** Modern Syne display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear bilingual taco cuts and pricing.
