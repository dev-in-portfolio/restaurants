## Original Audit Weakness

The Teal Turnip is one of Charlotte’s most acclaimed culinary gems, founded by chef Taylor Kastl and Steve McGinley at 1640 Oakhurst Commons Drive in Charlotte’s Oakhurst neighborhood. Known by the rallying cry "Fine Dining in a T-Shirt," The Teal Turnip combines rigorous French-trained culinary technique and hyper-local farm sourcing with a whimsical, pun-filled small plates menu, a serious bourbon cocktail bar, and an intentional adults-only sanctuary policy. Despite glowing reviews and devoted food-lover followings, third-party delivery aggregators and casual directories often mischaracterize it as a fast-casual sandwich shop or fail to communicate its seasonal menu rotation, chef pedigree, child-free dining policy, and curated spirit program.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Oakhurst Location:** 1640 Oakhurst Commons Dr, Suite 105, Charlotte, NC 28205.
2. **Operating Schedule:** Tuesday 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Live music & bar bites); Wednesday – Saturday 11:00 AM – 9:30 PM (Full service); Closed Sunday & Monday.
3. **Adults-Only Atmosphere:** A child-free dining establishment dedicated to intimate dining, conversation, and artisan cocktails.
4. **Signature Punny Small Plates:** "Duck Season" (sage-seared duck breast with parsnip purée and blackberry gastrique), "I'm Having a Lentil Breakdown" (Cajun-grilled salmon with smoked green lentil chili), "Belly Up to the Bar" (smoked pork belly with kohlrabi noodle salad), "I Yam What I Yam" (sweet potato bruschetta with lemon mascarpone), and "Don't You Wish Your Crab Dip was Hot Like Me".
5. **Bourbon & Cocktail Craft:** Handcrafted bourbon libations, nitrogen-infused cocktails, and regional small-batch spirits.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Oakhurst Teal & Turnip Root Palette:** Turnip root obsidian (`#0c1517`), deep ocean teal (`#133b40`), vibrant turnip teal (`#1f7375`), soft sage teal (`#429b96`), radish blush (`#c25974`), warm gold butter (`#e6af65`), and bone porcelain (`#f5f7f6`).
2. **Whimsical Culinary Typography:** Elegant literary serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and playful kitchen monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** Pan-seared scored duck breast medallion glazed in rich blackberry gastrique with confit leek towers and microgreens; pan-seared blackened salmon fillet over a bed of grain risotto and greens ringed with herb oil and cream reductions; and a classic handcrafted whiskey sour with lemon and orange wheels on the rocks.
