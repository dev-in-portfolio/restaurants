## Original Audit Weakness

Luce Ristorante represents the pinnacle of Italian fine dining in Uptown Charlotte, famous for its romantic Murano chandelier ambiance, exquisite Northern Italian cuisine, and coveted location across from the Blumenthal Performing Arts Center. However, its online presence was hosted on a shared group portal without an interactive sommelier wine guide, course-by-course pasta pairing details, or dedicated pre-theater reservation booking. This 6-page concept provides a luxurious, world-class digital showcase.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Artisanal Handmade Pastas & Risottos:** Tagliolini al tartufo with black truffles, wild boar ragù pappardelle, veal ravioli with sage butter, and saffron risotto Milanese.
2. **Secondi Piatti & Prime Cuts:** Slow-braised veal Osso Buco with gremolata, pan-seared Mediterranean sea bass (Branzino), and prime filet mignon in Barolo wine reduction.
3. **Venetian Ambiance & Pre-Theater Dining:** Handcrafted Murano glass chandelier, covered piazza terrace, and timed pre-theater dinner service.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Venetian Gold & Sangiovese Burgundy Palette:** Venetian Gold (`#d4af37`), Tuscan Sangiovese Burgundy (`#881337`), Italian Olive Marble (`#1c1917`), and Parchment Silk (`#faf7f2`).
2. **Fine Dining Visual Architecture:** Course-by-course Italian dining structure, sommelier wine cellar matrix, and Blumenthal showtime dining timeline.
3. **Luxurious Typography:** Cormorant Garamond serif display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for wine vintages.
