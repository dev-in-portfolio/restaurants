## Original Audit Weakness

Punta Cana relies on generic third-party delivery redirects and unbranded social listings. Located in the bustling South Boulevard international culinary district, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its authentic Dominican mofongo mortar craft, slow-roasted marinated pernil, daily lunch specials, and family celebration catering trays.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South Boulevard Location:** 5230 South Blvd, Charlotte NC 28217.
2. **Dominican & Caribbean Culinary Specialties:** Traditional Plantain Mofongo with Shrimp in Salsa Criolla, Crispy Slow-Roasted Pernil Asado, Rabo Encendido (Stewed Oxtail), Chivo Guisado (Braised Goat), Sweet Fried Maduros, Crispy Tostones, Morir Soñando, and Tropical Rum Cocktails.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 529-3599; Mon–Sat 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Lunch Specials 11 AM – 3 PM) | Sun 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Expressive Caribbean Heritage Serif Typography:** High-impact tropical serif (*DM Serif Display*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and South Blvd kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Caribbean Sapphire & Sunburst Mango Palette:** Sun-drenched island palette anchored in deep Caribbean sapphire (`#1E3A8A`), ocean slate (`#0F172A`), sunburst mango gold (`#F59E0B`), Dominican flag crimson (`#DC2626`), tropical palm mint (`#059669`), sweet plantain cream (`#FEF3C7`), and clean pure white.
3. **"The Dominican Mofongo & Pernil Asado Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`mofongo-pernil-and-caribbean-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing mortar-mashed mofongo, slow-roasted pernil, and rich Caribbean stews alongside tropical rum cocktails and fresh island juices.

### Divergent Choices for Punta Cana

- **Hero Composition:** Tropical Dominican Island Grill & Caribbean Palm Veranda split-hero layout: left side features a Dominican Palm Seal badge (`"AUTÉNTICA COCINA DOMINICANA & TROPICAL RUM BAR • SOUTH BLVD CHARLOTTE"`), expressive tropical display typography (*DM Serif Display*), Caribbean sapphire & sunburst mango palette, and right side features a framed hero image of seasoned Caribbean jerk platters with sweet plantains and red beans.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Punta Cana Vault Cards** (`puntacana-vault-card`) and South Boulevard Caribbean grill highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`puntacana-header`, `puntacana-brand`, `tropical-hero-stage`, `puntacana-seal-badge`, `puntacana-vault-card`, `puntacana-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
