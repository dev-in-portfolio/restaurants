## Original Audit Weakness

Seoul Food Meat Company is one of Charlotte's most energetic culinary institutions, blending slow-smoked Southern hickory pit barbecue with vibrant Korean street-food marinades and side dishes across two flagship campuses (South End and the Mill District). Featuring private karaoke rooms, adult playgrounds with outdoor lawn games, massive dog parks, and a late-night pocha cocktail experience, directory listings frequently overlook their sophisticated wood-smoking discipline, proprietary scratch gochujang glazes, and dual-campus operational logistics.
