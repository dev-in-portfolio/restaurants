## Original Audit Weakness

Spaghett is a distinguished 13-table craft pasta house and cocktail salon situated in the historic 1885 Young-Morrison House at 224 West 10th Street in Uptown Charlotte’s Fourth Ward. Founded on daily handmade extruded and folded pasta traditions, hyper-seasonal Carolina agricultural sourcing, an acclaimed spirit-forward bar program led by Amanda Britton, and a structured four-course Italian prix fixe progression (Primi, Pasta, Entrata, Dolci), it commands immense regional acclaim. However, generic restaurant indexes frequently fail to explain its intimate 13-table setting, reservation release cadence on Tock, walk-in bar accommodations, or seasonal course structure.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Historic Fourth Ward Location:** Located at 224 West 10th Street, Charlotte NC 28202 in the historic Young-Morrison House.
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Wednesday through Monday: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM (Closed Tuesdays).
3. **Four-Course Prix Fixe Experience:** Structured four-course culinary journey ($70–$75 per person) featuring Primi, Pasta, Entrata, and Dolci, updated regularly to reflect micro-seasonal farm arrivals.
4. **Handcrafted Daily Pasta Craft:** Daily extruded and hand-rolled pasta ribbons including Tagliatelle with braised pancetta ragù, Penne with San Marzano pomodoro and pecorino, agnolotti, and delicate filled culurgiones.
5. **Acclaimed Cocktail & Amaro Salon:** Bar program curated by Amanda Britton featuring the namesake Spaghett cocktail, amaro flights, paper planes, negroni variations, and low-intervention Italian wines.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Victorian Brick & Tuscan Saffron Palette:** Deep heritage charcoal (`#121110`), warm antique brick card (`#1c1815`), rich saffron pasta gold (`#e5a93c`), Florentine terracotta (`#c85a32`), and aged parchment white (`#f9f7f2`).
2. **Refined Classical Typography:** Timeless display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean modern sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced culinary notes (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Italian Culinary Photography:** Golden ribbons of hand-rolled tagliatelle with pancetta and fresh basil, penne pasta tossed in rich San Marzano pomodoro with pecorino, and a classic amaro paper plane cocktail in a stemmed crystal coupe.
