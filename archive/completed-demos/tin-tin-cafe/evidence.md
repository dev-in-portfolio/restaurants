## Original Audit Weakness

Tin Tin Café is a beloved neighborhood Japanese sushi and hibachi bistro situated at 3020 Driwood Court in North Charlotte / University City (NC 28269). Known across the community for generous portions, high-grade sashimi, sizzling hibachi steaks and seafood, and house-made yum yum sauce, the restaurant relied on an outdated Bootstrap 3 single-page template with broken external order links, low-contrast typography, and missing dedicated culinary showcases for its hand-rolled specialty sushi rolls and sizzling griddle combinations.
