## Original Audit Weakness

Sheng Ramen is Ballantyne’s premier authentic Japanese noodle house and izakaya, located at 15201 John J Delaney Drive in South Charlotte. Known for its 16-hour simmered pork bone tonkotsu, torched rolled chashu, and scratch pan-fried gyoza, third-party delivery platforms routinely list Sheng Ramen with low-resolution stock bowl photos or generic Asian food tags that fail to capture the rigorous broth-simmering craft, custom alkaline noodle formulations, or comfortable izakaya dining environment in Ballantyne.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Ballantyne / South Charlotte Location:** Located at 15201 John J Delaney Drive, Suite B, Charlotte NC 28277. Phone: `(704) 919-0118`.
2. **16-Hour Simmered Broths:** Tonkotsu (original, spicy, and black garlic), Chicken Paitan, fermented Miso, Curry Ramen, and Vegetarian Tofu Broths.
3. **Noodle & Topping Discipline:** Firm artisan alkaline ramen noodles, torched rolled pork chashu, jammy ajitsuke tamago eggs, wood ear kikurage, and menma bamboo shoots.
4. **Izakaya Small Plates & Rice Bowls:** Crispy pan-fried gyoza with golden lace skirts, ginger-soy Japanese fried chicken karaage, pork belly bao buns, Chashu Don, and Chicken Katsu Don.
5. **Operating Schedule:** Mon–Thu: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM & 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM; Fri–Sat: 11:00 AM – 9:30 PM; Sun: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Ramen Shop Obsidian & Crimson Lacquer Palette:** Deep charcoal broth obsidian (`#111113`), dark wood dining card (`#1b1a18`), Japanese vermilion red (`#c93b2b`), ramen egg gold (`#e5a93c`), and steam ivory (`#fcfaf6`).
2. **Clean Noodle House Typography:** Classical serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced broth simmer timers (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Ramen & Izakaya Photography:** Steaming Tonkotsu ramen with torched chashu and jammy egg in stone bowl, pan-fried gyoza with crispy skirts, and Japanese chicken karaage on perilla leaves.
