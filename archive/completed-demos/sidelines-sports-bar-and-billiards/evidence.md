## Original Audit Weakness

Sidelines Sports Bar & Billiards has been an anchor of Charlotte’s Lower South End (LoSo) corridor at 4544 South Boulevard since 2007. Celebrated as the Carolinas’ premier inclusive LGBTQ+ sports bar, Sidelines features four regulation tournament pool tables, steel-tip dartboards, multiple HDTV screens broadcasting live sports, and a welcoming outdoor patio. However, third-party directory listings frequently misclassify it as a nightclub or display outdated daytime photos, failing to showcase its relaxed game-day atmosphere, weekly billiards leagues, or dedicated community sports bar culture.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Lower South End Location:** Located at 4544 South Blvd, Charlotte NC 28209. Phone: `(704) 525-2608`.
2. **Weekly Operating Hours:** Mon–Thu: 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM; Fri: 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sat: 2:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sun: 12:00 PM – 12:00 AM.
3. **Four Tournament Pool Tables & Steel-Tip Darts:** Four 10-foot and 8-foot regulation slate billiard tables, free pool nights, steel-tip dartboards, foosball, and video lounge.
4. **Sports Broadcasts & Game-Day Hub:** Wall-to-wall HDTVs broadcasting NFL, college football, soccer, hockey, and basketball with sound on for marquee matches.
5. **Inclusive Sports Haven Since 2007:** Proudly providing a safe, ultra-friendly, and mature gathering environment for sports enthusiasts and community leagues.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Sports Lounge Cobalt & Stadium Gold Palette:** Deep stadium obsidian (`#0f1115`), sports lounge navy card (`#161a22`), electric cobalt blue (`#1e50ff`), stadium amber gold (`#f5a623`), and stadium white (`#f4f6fa`).
2. **Bold Modern Sports Typography:** Refined bold serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced match specs (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Billiards & Sports Bar Photography:** Macro billiard balls scattered on grey tournament felt, precision steel-tip darts in cork dartboard, and refreshing highball cocktail with lime and mint.
