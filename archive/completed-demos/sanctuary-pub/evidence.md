## Original Audit Weakness

The Sanctuary is NoDa’s quintessential and longest-standing neighborhood dive bar, located at 507 East 36th Street in the historic heart of Charlotte’s arts district since 1990. Open 365 days a year until 2:00 AM, the bar is celebrated for its classic pool tables, retro jukebox, cheap cold beers, shot specials, and welcoming patio. However, third-party directories often misclassify it as a generic Irish pub or fail to showcase its thirty-year community history, billiards culture, or late-night hospitality directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Historic NoDa Location:** Located at 507 East 36th Street, Charlotte NC 28205 at the corner of 36th and North Davidson. Phone: `(704) 333-0346`.
2. **Open 365 Days a Year Until 2 AM:** Mon–Fri 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sat 2:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sun 12:00 PM – 2:00 AM.
3. **Billiards & Retro Jukebox Culture:** Regulation pool tables with nightly casual games and neighborhood league tournaments, retro jukebox, and laid-back outdoor front patio.
4. **Drinks & Tavern Fare:** Cold macro tallboys, local Charlotte IPAs, bourbon shot specials, hot tavern bar pizza, warm Bavarian pretzels with spicy mustard, and salty bar snacks.
5. **Community Sanctuary Vibe:** An unpretentious haven for artists, musicians, service industry workers, and neighborhood regulars since 1990.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Tavern Amber & Emerald Felt Palette:** Deep tavern charcoal (`#121214`), dark oak wood card (`#1c1a17`), pool table emerald green (`#2d6a4f`), neon amber gold (`#e9c46a`), and warm parchment (`#f8f6f0`).
2. **Classic American Tavern Typography:** Refined bold serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced hours/specs (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Tavern Photography:** Cold craft beer with frothy head on a rustic wooden tavern table with bar pizza, emerald felt billiard balls racked under pool lights, and neat bourbon pour in Glencairn glass.

### Divergent Choices for Sanctuary Pub

- **Hero Composition:** Warm tavern split-hero with 36th Street kicker (`"NODA HISTORIC ARTS DISTRICT • 507 E 36TH ST • OPEN 365 DAYS UNTIL 2 AM"`), emerald green and amber gold accents, and a framed hero shot of a cold craft beer and bar pizza on rustic oak, anchored by a floating hero badge (`NoDa Neighborhood Dive | 507 E 36th St • Pool & Pints Since 1990`).
- **Section Rhythm:** Three-card standards grid without emojis highlighting "Regulation Pool Tables", "Cold Pints & Shot Specials", and "Open 365 Days A Year", followed by dual alternating highlight banners.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Bespoke classes (`sanctuary-header`, `sanctuary-brand`, `sanctuary-hero-stage`, `sanctuary-hero-badge`, `sanctuary-standards-section`, `sanctuary-standard-card`, `sanctuary-highlight-banner`, `sanctuary-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
