## Original Audit Weakness

Zio Casual Italian is one of Charlotte’s most beloved and romantic neighborhood restaurants, nestled at 116 Middleton Drive on the quiet residential edge of Myers Park and Eastover. Renowned for its signature grilled thin-crust pizzas, scratch pastas, legendary toasted ravioli, hand-rolled jumbo meatballs, stuffed banana peppers, and enchanting garden patio bordered by fresh herbs, Zio feels like a hidden Italian village trattoria. However, its online presence is fragmented across dated website templates and third-party waitlist widgets that do not communicate its intimate dinner hours, garden patio policies, or scratch Italian wine program effectively.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Physical Address:** 116 Middleton Dr, Charlotte, NC 28207 (Historic Myers Park / Eastover).
2. **Contact Phone:** (704) 344-0101.
3. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Monday – Thursday 5:00 PM – 9:30 PM; Friday – Saturday 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM; Sunday 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM (Dinner service only).
4. **Artisan Grilled Pizzas & Scratch Pastas:** Signature grilled margherita pizza with charred blistered edges, angel hair & hand-rolled meatballs, baked lasagna, penne alla vodka, and chicken parmesan.
5. **Beloved Trattoria Antipasti:** Hand-breaded crispy toasted ravioli with marinara, stuffed banana peppers with sausage, baby arugula salad, and Tuscan wine pairings.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Florentine Terracotta & Tuscan Olive Palette:** Deep charred espresso (`#151311`), rustic brick terracotta (`#b84d32`), warm olive grove green (`#3d5236`), toasted semolina cream (`#faf6ed`), warm travertine (`#e8dfce`), and chianti crimson (`#8c2d28`).
2. **Elegant Italian Trattoria Typography:** Romantic classical serif (*Playfair Display* / *Cinzel*) paired with clean legible sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and wine cellar monospace (*DM Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A blistered, wood-kissed grilled margherita pizza with fresh buffalo mozzarella and torn basil; a wide pasta bowl of spaghetti in rich marinara with two large hand-rolled meatballs and shaved parmigiano; and golden crispy toasted ravioli with pecorino and house tomato dipping sauce.
