## Original Audit Weakness

El Taco Deli is an East Charlotte staple on Albemarle Road, beloved for its fresh Mexican street tacos, hearty breakfast platters, and authentic elotes locos. However, the business lacked an authoritative, owned digital home, relying solely on fragmented third-party delivery portal stubs that charge heavy commission fees and miss the craft story behind their morning breakfasts, house-marinated meats, and scratch salsas. This 6-page concept provides an authoritative, bilingual web platform highlighting their complete menu and direct phone pickup line.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Street Tacos & Quesabirria:** Double-tortilla street tacos loaded with Carne Asada, slow-cooked Barbacoa, Al Pastor, and griddled Quesabirria with rich red dipping consomé.
2. **All-Day Mexican Breakfast:** Huevos a la Mexicana with scrambled eggs, fresh tomato, jalapeño, and onion, served with refried beans, avocado, and sweet fried plantains.
3. **Elotes Locos & Street Antojitos:** Classic Mexican street corn on the cob slathered in crema, cotija cheese, and chili powder, alongside fresh beef/chicken empanadas and crispy churros.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Salsa Roja & Sunset Amber Palette:** Salsa Roja Red (`#b7094c`), Sunset Amber Gold (`#ff9e00`), Lime Verde Green (`#38b000`), and Warm Tortilla Beige (`#fbf9f4`).
2. **Taqueria Deli Layout Architecture:** Clean breakfast-to-dinner combo matrices with clear meat options, salsa pairings, and side choices.
3. **Bold Energetic Typography:** High-contrast Outfit display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear bilingual descriptions and prices.
