## Original Audit Weakness

Tyber Creek Pub is a legendary Charlotte Irish pub operating for over 25 years in South End. Having recently relocated to 1501 S Mint Street in the vibrant Gold District, the pub preserved its authentic original mahogany bar, historic pub signs, European football morning screenings, and genuine community hospitality. The menu features proper Irish pub classics—including beer-battered Icelandic cod & chips ($18), the Dublin spice bag ($14), curry chips ($10), double smash burgers ($16), corned beef reubens ($16), and perfectly poured 20oz imperial pints of Guinness, Harp, Smithwick's, and Magners cider. However, its web presence was anchored on an outdated single-page SpotHopper template with buried food menus, unclear relocation context, and no dedicated pages for match day screenings, private pub gatherings, or patio dining.
