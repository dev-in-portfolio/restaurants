## Original Audit Weakness

Oh My Ganache relies on basic social media pages and third-party delivery platforms. Located at 270 Camp Rd at Camp North End, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its interactive build-your-own cheesecake bar experience, Southern-inspired bakery fusion creations, catering boxes, and Camp North End visitor directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Camp North End Charlotte Location:** 270 Camp Rd, Suite 103, Charlotte NC 28206 (Mount District at Camp North End).
2. **Artisan Bakery & Cheesecake Specialties:** Build-Your-Own Cheesecake Bar (velvety vanilla cheesecake bases on butter graham crusts with scratch toppings), Sweet Potato Pie Cheesecake, Warm Peach Cobbler Bread Pudding, Bourbon Caramel Apple Slices, Dark Chocolate Ganache Drizzles, & Custom Cheesecake Flights.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Wed–Thu 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Fri–Sat 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Sun 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM | Mon–Tue Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Luxurious Haute-Pâtisserie Serif Typography:** Elegant Parisian chic display serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and pastry chef ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Dark Cocoa Ganache & Raspberry Velvet Palette:** Indulgent confectionery palette anchored in dark cocoa ganache truffle (`#2E151B`), raspberry velvet rose (`#E11D48`), salted caramel gold (`#D97706`), and warm vanilla cream (`#FFF7ED`).
3. **"The Custom Cheesecake Bar & Southern Bakery Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`custom-cheesecake-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing build-your-own cheesecake slices alongside Southern bakery specialties and dessert flights.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Oaklore Bar & Bottle Shop` — Charred Oak & Copper Distillery with DM Serif Display fonts and Charred Oak/Copper.
2. `Nora Mac’s Traditional Irish Pub` — Dublin Celtic Public House with Cinzel fonts and Stout Onyx/Shamrock Emerald.
3. `NoDa Bodega` — Bohemian Arts Deli with Bungee fonts and Forest Olive/Mustard Relish.

### Divergent Choices for Oh My Ganache

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Artisan Confectionery & Camp North End Ganache Bakery Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Gourmet Ganache Stamp badge (`"CUSTOM ARTISAN CHEESECAKE BAR & SOUTHERN BAKERY • CAMP NORTH END CHARLOTTE"`), elegant Parisian display typography (*Playfair Display*), dark cocoa ganache & raspberry rose palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of golden layered cheesecakes with warm caramel and berry compote drizzles.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Oh My Ganache Vault Cards** (`ganache-vault-card`) and Charlotte Camp North End dessert highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`ganache-header`, `ganache-brand`, `bakery-hero-stage`, `ganache-seal-badge`, `ganache-vault-card`, `ganache-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
