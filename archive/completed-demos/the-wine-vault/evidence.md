## Original Audit Weakness

The Wine Vault is Charlotte's premier wine bar, bottle shop, and lakefront boardwalk destination located at 9009-1 J.M. Keynes Drive in University Place (with an additional location on Hawthorne Lane in Elizabeth). Celebrated for over 1,000 global wine labels, rotating craft beers on tap, its signature Wednesday $9 six-wine tasting flights, gourmet cheese & charcuterie spreads, and a scenic dog-friendly waterfront patio with live acoustic music, its current website is hosted on an outdated Squarespace template with generic layouts, minimal wine flight visibility, and no interactive event booking.
