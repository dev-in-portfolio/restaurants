## Original Audit Weakness

SWIRL Dessert Bar is South End Charlotte’s legendary dessert lounge located at 2822 South Boulevard, Unit B. Renowned as the world’s first made-to-order custom cupcake bar, SWIRL revolutionized the bakery experience by allowing guests to build their cupcakes from scratch—selecting baked bases, injected core fillings, piped-to-order buttercreams, and artisan toppings. Online scrapers frequently reduce it to a generic ice cream shop or dry pastry counter, missing its made-to-order interactive counter, loaded artisan milkshakes ("Bad Decision", "Fake ID"), scratch-baked cookies, and late-night South End dessert culture.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Charlotte Location:** 2822 South Boulevard, Unit B, Charlotte, NC 28209.
2. **Evening & Late-Night Hours:** Mon–Thu 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM, Fri 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM, Sat 2:00 PM – 11:00 PM, Sun 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM.
3. **World’s First Made-to-Order Cupcake Bar:** Select fresh cake base (vanilla, dark chocolate, red velvet, funfetti, GF), core filling (salted caramel, marshmallow, raspberry, fudge), piped-to-order frosting (cream cheese, Nutella, cookies & cream, peanut butter), and gourmet toppings.
4. **Signature Artisan Milkshakes:** Loaded creations including "Bad Decision" (vanilla, Oreos, raspberry puree), "Tommy" (fruity cereal milk, marshmallow fluff), "Fake ID" (chocolate, fudge brownie, peanut butter cups), and "Made to Last" (cookie dough, espresso, salted caramel).
5. **Fresh-Baked Cookies & Party Cakes:** Warm scratch cookies (Salted Oreo, Churro, Chocolate Chip) and custom celebration cookie cakes.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Vibrant South End Confectionery Palette:** Deep berry midnight (`#18121a`), confection parlor slate (`#261d2b`), frosted strawberry glaze (`#e04f7c`), buttercream champagne (`#f5d372`), and vanilla cream (`#fdf9f5`).
2. **Playful Modern Bakery Typography:** Heritage display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with sleek modern sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced recipe tags (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Confectionery Photography:** Piped chocolate buttercream cupcakes with colorful candy sprinkles on a glass pedestal; loaded strawberry swirl milkshake with whipped cream; and thick freshly baked chocolate chip cookies.
