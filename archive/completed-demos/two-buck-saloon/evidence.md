## Original Audit Weakness

Two Buck Saloon is Charlotte's iconic unpretentious neighborhood dive bar founded at 1113 Hawthorne Lane in Belmont / Plaza Midwood by Rob Rivers & Mark Spoto, now expanded across Greater Charlotte (Hawthorne, Oakhurst, Mint Hill, Pineville, Locust, Shelby). Known for honest $2 PBRs, the signature $5 "Buck Shot" (pint of PBR + shot of Jim Beam), free pool tables, steel-tip dart boards, jukebox tunes, and a welcoming BYOF (Bring Your Own Food) policy, its existing website is built on a generic Duda/IRP web builder with slow load speeds, unstyled layout drawers, missing dedicated pages for its BYOF traditions and gaming culture, and low conversion clarity.
