## Original Audit Weakness

Rai Lay Thai relies on generic third-party delivery redirects and unbranded social listings. Located in prime South End along South Blvd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its Southern Thai coastal gastronomy, Krabi-inspired limestone and ocean aesthetics, wok-fired street noodle mastery, and South End takeout express directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Charlotte Location:** 1520 South Blvd, Suite 130, Charlotte NC 28203 (in the heart of South End near the Rail Trail).
2. **Southern Thai Coastal Offerings:** Wok-Fired Pad Thai, Drunken Noodles (Pad Kee Mao), Massaman Beef Curry, Panang Curry with Kaffir Lime, Crispy Duck Tamarind, Tom Yum Goong, Sweet Mango Sticky Rice, Iced Thai Milk Tea, and Craft Asian Cocktails.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (980) 207-0991; Tue–Fri 11:00 AM – 2:45 PM & 5:00 PM – 9:20 PM | Sat 12:00 PM – 9:20 PM | Sun 5:00 PM – 9:20 PM | Mon Closed.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Elegant Coastal Resort Serif Typography:** Classical editorial serif (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and South End kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Andaman Sea Teal & Phuket Gold Palette:** Coastal Thailand luxury palette anchored in deep Andaman teal (`#0F3E3D`), midnight Krabi tide (`#042F2E`), Phuket sunburst gold (`#F59E0B`), fresh lemongrass mint (`#10B981`), jasmine coconut cream (`#F0FDF4`), and clean pure white.
3. **"The Southern Thai Coastal Curries & Wok Noodles Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`southern-thai-curries-and-wok-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing coconut curries, high-heat wok noodles, and seafood specialties alongside artisan Thai iced teas and craft cocktails.

- **Hero Composition:** Southern Thai Coastal Sanctuary & Krabi Cliff Lagoon split-hero layout: left side features a Railay Beach Coastal Seal badge (`"KRABI COASTAL THAI GASTRONOMY • WOK-FIRED NOODLES & CURRIES • SOUTH END CHARLOTTE"`), refined coastal display typography (*Cormorant Garamond*), deep Andaman teal & Phuket gold palette, and right side features a framed hero image of wok-fired shrimp pad thai.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Railay Vault Cards** (`railay-vault-card`) and South End Rail Trail dining highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`railay-header`, `railay-brand`, `andaman-hero-stage`, `railay-seal-badge`, `railay-vault-card`, `railay-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
