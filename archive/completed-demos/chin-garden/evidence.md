## Original Audit Weakness

Chin Garden is an established, dependable Chinese culinary anchor for East Charlotte and The Plaza neighborhood, known for high-heat wok dishes and generous combination platters. However, the restaurant lacks an official owned website, forcing customers to rely on third-party aggregators with outdated pricing, missing combo descriptions, and unverified hours. This 6-page concept provides an authoritative, modern digital home highlighting their wok heritage, complete combination platters, chef's specials, and quick phone pickup guide.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **High-Heat Wok Hei Mastery:** High-temperature flame stir-frying with fresh vegetables, premium cuts of beef and poultry, and made-from-scratch brown garlic and spicy chili sauces.
2. **Beloved Chinese Takeout Classics:** Crispy General Tso’s Chicken with chili pods, golden Sesame Chicken, Beef with Broccoli, Shrimp Lo Mein, curry-spiced Singapore Chow Mei Fun, Shrimp Egg Foo Young, and Bar-B-Q Spare Ribs.
3. **All-Day Value Combination Platters:** Every combo served with roast pork fried rice and a golden crispy egg roll.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Imperial Crimson & Wok Amber Palette:** Imperial Crimson Red (`#9b2226`), Wok Fire Amber (`#ee9b00`), Lacquered Charcoal (`#151618`), and Bamboo Cream (`#f8f6f0`).
2. **Combination Platter Matrix Cards:** Clear structured cards for combos highlighting default sides, lunch hours, and protein choices.
3. **Bold Artisan Typography:** Syne / Cinzel modern bold display paired with Plus Jakarta Sans body and DM Mono for quick pricing and combination codes.
