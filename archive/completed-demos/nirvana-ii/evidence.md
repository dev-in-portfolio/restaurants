## Original Audit Weakness

Nirvana II is an essential weekday lunch destination for thousands of Uptown Charlotte banking and tech professionals seeking authentic Indian flavors in Two Wells Fargo Center. Despite high demand for its rapid-fire curry combos and clay-oven naan, the restaurant suffered from split online branding between "Nirvana II" and "Nirvana Indian Fusion" across delivery portals without a responsive, beautiful web showcase detailing daily biryani batches, corporate thali catering, or custom curry pairings. This 6-page concept provides an authentic, high-speed digital home.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **"Lunch in a Hurry" Thali Combos:** Fragrant chicken or vegetable biryani served with choice of 2 slow-simmered curries, hot garlic butter naan or chapati, and chilled cucumber raita.
2. **Clay Oven Tandoori & Freshly Slapped Naan:** Marinated tandoori chicken roasted over intense clay embers, paired with blistered garlic naan brushed with pure ghee.
3. **Heritage Spice Gravies:** Velvety fenugreek tomato makhani, spiced creamed spinach palak paneer, and slow-braised lamb curries.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Saffron & Terracotta Crimson Palette:** Saffron Gold (`#f59e0b`), Tandoor Terracotta Crimson (`#dc2626`), Cardamom Forest Green (`#15803d`), and Warm Basmati Silk (`#fdfbf7`).
2. **Thali Combo Visual Architecture:** 2-curry selection matrix, spice heat guides, and office boxed lunch catering layouts.
3. **Exotic Modern Typography:** Cinzel display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for quick lunch combo ordering.
