## Original Audit Weakness

Cedars Halal Market, Restaurant & Hookah is a beloved cultural cornerstone on East Charlotte's bustling Central Avenue corridor, serving authentic Levantine dishes and supplying specialty halal groceries to the Queen City. However, the business operates without an owned canonical website, forcing diners and grocery shoppers to rely on outdated directory stubs, unverified third-party delivery menus, and fragmented social check-ins. This demo establishes a unified, beautiful digital home that presents the kitchen, the market, and the hookah lounge with clarity and prestige.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **The Triple-Concept Anchor:** Seamless integration of a hot halal Mediterranean grill, a fully stocked Middle Eastern market pantry & butcher, and a relaxed shisha hookah lounge.
2. **Signature Levantine Heritage Dishes:** Verified house specialties including spiced chicken shawarma, charbroiled shish tawook and kofta kabobs, Turkish potato kibbeh, Turkish lahmajun, freshly griddled manoushe, arayes, and syrup-soaked knafeh.
3. **The Central Avenue Community Pantry:** Authentic imported goods from across the Mediterranean—za'atar, sumac, cold-pressed olive oils, pomegranate molasses, basmati rice, Turkish coffees, Vimto, and fresh halal meats.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Mediterranean Cedar & Terracotta Visual Palette:** Deep Forest Cedar Green (`#1b3b2b`), Warm Mediterranean Terracotta (`#c85a32`), Golden Saffron (`#e5a93c`), and Sandstone Cream (`#faf6f0`), evoking the warmth of Mediterranean family tables.
2. **Classical Arched Framing & Geometric Lattice Motifs:** Elegant architectural arch frames for key hero imagery and structured tabbed sections for Kitchen, Market, and Hookah.
3. **Warm Editorial Typography System:** Marcellus classical display font paired with Plus Jakarta Sans body and DM Mono for precision pricing and provenance callouts.
