## Original Audit Weakness

The Gin Mill South End is an iconic, multi-level neighborhood gathering place located at 1423 South Tryon Street, directly in front of the historic Amos' Southend music venue. Blending the relaxed charm of a classic American tavern downstairs with an expansive second-story open-air rooftop bar overlooking the South Tryon streetscape and Uptown skyline, The Gin Mill delivers scratch-made pub fare including the signature double "Mill Burger", the fiery "Psycho Burger", jumbo slow-smoked and fried chicken wings, loaded beer cheese tots, and crispy flatbreads. Despite its status as one of South End’s most enduring nightlife and game-day institutions, third-party directory listings frequently misidentify the physical address as 1411 South Tryon (the former adjacent footprint) or conflate it with unrelated bar ventures, omitting its full scratch kitchen menu, weekend daytime hours, rooftop deck capacity, and community events schedule.

5. **Rooftop Deck & Tavern Atmosphere:** Second-floor open-air terrace with skyline views, craft beer draft lines, premium gin & tonic highballs, trivia nights, and game day sound.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Tavern Brick & Copper Rooftop Palette:** South End tavern charcoal (`#141518`), brick tavern crimson (`#a33b31`), warm copper gold (`#d9923b`), rooftop dusk slate (`#262830`), weathered pine wood (`#1f2127`), and parchment linen (`#f5f4ef`).
2. **Industrial Tavern Typography:** Bold athletic slab serif (*Alfa Slab One* / *Bebas Neue*) paired with rugged contemporary sans (*Inter*) and industrial terminal monospace (*DM Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A loaded double bacon cheeseburger with melted cheddar and golden French fries on a rectangular white platter; crispy buffalo chicken wings with fresh celery and blue cheese dip; and an effervescent gin and tonic cocktail in a faceted crystal highball glass garnished with a fresh green lime wedge.
