## Original Audit Weakness

Deli HN is an East Charlotte culinary gem celebrated for authentic Honduran street food, charcoal-grilled carne asada, and crispy Pollo con Tajadas. However, the business operates without an official owned website, relying only on fragmented delivery app listings that lack background on their San Pedro Sula recipes, handmade tortilla process, and outdoor lot pickup instructions. This 6-page concept establishes an authoritative bilingual digital showcase highlighting their asador heritage, generous platters, and weekend hours.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **San Pedro Sula Style Asados & Pollo con Tajadas:** Charcoal-grilled marinated flank steak, pork chops, and crispy spiced chicken served over a bed of fried green plantain slices (tajadas de guineo verde), shredded cabbage slaw, chimol, and traditional pink dressing.
2. **Handmade Flour Baleadas:** Freshly stretched thick flour tortillas cooked on hot griddles, filled with refried Honduran red silk beans, rich mantequilla blanca, and grated dry cheese.
3. **East Charlotte Community Hub:** Located at 4405 The Plaza with outdoor plaza seating and weekend late-night service until 11:00 PM.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Maya Blue & Caribbean Gold Palette:** Honduran Maya Blue (`#0077b6`), Caribbean Sun Gold (`#f4a261`), Palm Leaf Green (`#2a9d8f`), and Charcoal Grill (`#181a1c`).
2. **Bilingual Street Food Storytelling:** English and Spanish headers celebrating authentic Honduran culinary terms (Tajadas, Chimol, Mantequilla Catracha, Horchata de Morro).
3. **Bold Energetic Typography:** High-contrast display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear portion and combo pricing.
