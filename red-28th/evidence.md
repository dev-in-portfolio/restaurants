## Original Audit Weakness

Red@28th has historically relied on fragmented social media announcements and third-party delivery portal cards following its relocation to University City. As Charlotte's landmark Black-owned multicultural literary lounge and hookah bar, it lacked an owned web presence communicating its warm living room atmosphere, floor-to-ceiling book library, organic coconut coal hookah program, comfort soul kitchen, and weekly open mic culture.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **University City Location:** 3509 David Cox Rd, Charlotte NC 28269. Situated in North Charlotte's University City cultural district with ample lot parking and outdoor patio seating.
2. **Offerings & Cultural Program:** Artisanal natural shisha blends with organic coconut coals, fresh fruit head carves (pineapple, watermelon), signature chicken wings (honey hot, lemon pepper, sweet chili), seasoned waffle fries, comfort sliders, handcrafted book-inspired cocktails, board games, and poetry open mic nights.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 910-6816; Mon–Thu: 4:00 PM – 1:00 AM | Fri–Sat: 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM | Sun: 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM. Ages 21+ after 8:00 PM with photo ID.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Harlem Renaissance Burgundy & Warm Amber Glow Palette:** Deep literary crimson (`#881337`), warm tobacco brown (`#291811`), ambient lamplight amber (`#F59E0B`), warm parchment cream (`#FEF3C7`), and deep espresso obsidian (`#120907`).
2. **Literary Serif & Warm Modern Display Typography:** Elegant literary display serif (*Lora*) paired with approachable humanistic sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and reading-room metadata mono (*Space Mono*).
3. **Bespoke Atmospheric Photography:** Floor-to-ceiling library living room with vintage velvet armchairs and patrons enjoying conversation, close-up tongs placing glowing red coconut charcoals on the hookah head, and glistening wings with seasoned waffle fries and cocktails on rustic wood.

### Divergent Choices for Red@28th

- **Hero Composition:** Warm literary salon split layout: left side features a Red@28th Heritage Kicker (`"CHARLOTTE'S ORIGINAL MULTICULTURAL LITERARY LOUNGE & HOOKAH BAR"`), rich literary serif typography (*Lora*), and warm amber buttons; right side features a framed hero image of the living-room library with floor-to-ceiling bookshelves and velvet armchairs, anchored by a floating badge (`University City | 3509 David Cox Rd • Books & Hookah`).
- **Section Rhythm:** Distinctive 3-card standards grid without emojis highlighting "Multicultural Literary Salon", "Artisanal Hookah Program", and "Late-Night Comfort Kitchen", followed by two rich alternating highlight banners.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Bespoke classes (`red-header`, `red-brand`, `red-hero-stage`, `red-hero-badge`, `red-standards-section`, `red-standard-card`, `red-highlight-banner`, `red-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
