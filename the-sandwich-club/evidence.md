## Original Audit Weakness

Since 1996, The Sandwich Club has been an Uptown Charlotte staple, renowned for its massive numbered roster of over 30 handcrafted sandwiches and lightning-speed lunch counter. However, their online presence remained fragmented across directory snippets without a dedicated multi-page website showcasing the complete 30-sandwich lineup, breakfast ordering guide, or corporate office catering sheets. This 6-page concept gives the restaurant a premier, high-energy digital storefront.

---

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **30+ Signature Sandwiches:** Massive triple-decker roasted turkey and bacon clubs, hot pressed focaccia paninis, griddled corned beef reubens on marble rye, and garden veggie supreme subs.
2. **Morning Breakfast Club:** Flaky breakfast croissants, egg & cheese bagels, and fresh roasted coffee served starting at 7:00 AM.
3. **Executive Office Catering:** Individual boxed sandwich lunches with kettle chips, pickle spear, and fresh baked chocolate chip cookies.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Mustard Gold & Deli Forest Green Palette:** Club Mustard Gold (`#d97706`), Deli Forest Green (`#15803d`), Tryon Brick Burgundy (`#991b1b`), and Parchment Kraft Cream (`#faf8f5`).
2. **30-Sandwich Visual Architecture:** Numbered sandwich board (#1 to #30), triple-decker layering callouts, and 10:50 AM lunch rush guide.
3. **Crisp Bold Typography:** Outfit display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for swift ordering clarity.
