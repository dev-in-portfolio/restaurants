## Original Audit Weakness

Town Brewing Company is Wesley Heights’ beloved community brewery and scratch taproom kitchen, located at 800 Grandin Road in Charlotte’s historic West End. Anchored in an airy industrial warehouse with exposed brick, steel beams, and a welcoming neighborhood patio, Town Brewing is acclaimed for its innovative craft brews (such as Escape Plan West Coast IPA and Cruise to Barbados sour) paired with an inventive kitchen serving hand-stretched artisan flatbreads, Texas Red's beer cheese nachos, and jumbo salted pretzels. Despite steady local acclaim as a West End social cornerstone, third-party brewery directories often present Town Brewing as a bare-bones beer tasting room, completely overlooking its full scratch culinary menu, artisan flatbread program, cocktail offerings, and pet-friendly community lawn.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Wesley Heights Location:** 800 Grandin Road, Charlotte, NC 28208.
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Tuesday – Sunday 12:00 PM – 10:00 PM; Closed Monday.
3. **Core Craft Drafts:** Escape Plan West Coast IPA (6.7%), Cruise to Barbados fruited sour (4.8%), Slow Punch German-style Helles lager (4.8%), and four-pour custom tasting flights served on wooden paddles.
4. **Artisan Flatbreads & Scratch Bites:** "Hot N' Bothered" flatbread (pepperoni, hot honey drizzle, pickled jalapenos, melted mozzarella), "The Big Chicken" buffalo/BBQ flatbread, Texas Red's beer cheese nachos, and warm jumbo soft pretzels with house beer cheese.
5. **Community Taproom Experience:** Dog-friendly outdoor patio, indoor communal tables, board games, run club meetups, and local Carolina craft cocktails.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Wesley Heights Industrial & Hops Palette:** Brewery basalt (`#111518`), industrial brick charcoal (`#1b2126`), mill timber (`#272a2e`), golden amber hop (`#d9923b`), hop hover (`#ba7828`), fresh pine green (`#3d7a5a`), and natural barley linen (`#f5f4ef`).
2. **Industrial Taproom Typography:** Bold architectural serif (*Cinzel*) paired with warm contemporary sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and brewery batch monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A wood-fired artisan flatbread pizza cut into diagonal wedges with bubbly melted mozzarella, sun-dried tomatoes, and fresh herbs on a wooden peel; a towering platter of Texas Red's nachos with beer cheese, jalapenos, scallions, and sour cream; and a four-glass craft beer flight on a wooden paddle.
