## Original Audit Weakness

Doña Ana Deli & Restaurant is a cherished culinary anchor for the East Charlotte Hispanic community, celebrated for authentic Dominican comfort food, generous hot-table portions, and traditional breakfasts. However, their web presence was limited to an unmaintained legacy placeholder lacking complete daily specials, catering platter packages, breakfast hours, and clear store location directions. This 6-page concept provides an authoritative, bilingual digital home celebrating their Caribbean recipes, hot-table buffet, and family catering.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Slow-Roasted Pernil & Daily Guisados:** Traditional Dominican pernil marinated with garlic, oregano, and sour orange, roasted slow until falling off the bone with crispy skin, served alongside savory Pollo Guisado and Chivo Liniero.
2. **Mangú con Los Tres Golpes:** Morning staple of butter-mashed green plantains topped with tangy pickled red onions, thick slices of fried Dominican salami, sunny fried eggs, and golden fried tropical cheese (queso de freír).
3. **Mofongo & Party Picaderas:** Garlic mashed fried plantains in wooden pilones and festive catering party platters with mini empanadas, quippes, tostones, and fried pork longaniza.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Dominican Royal Blue & Caribe Coral Palette:** Dominican Royal Blue (`#0038a8`), Caribe Coral Red (`#ce1126`), Plantain Amber Gold (`#ffb703`), and Warm Cocoa Cream (`#fdf8f2`).
2. **Hot-Table Buffet & Combo Layout:** Clear meal combination cards detailing protein choices, rice & beans (moro de gandules), and plantain sides.
3. **Bold Caribbean Typography:** High-contrast Syne display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear bilingual item translations and portion sizes.
