## Original Audit Weakness

Central Tea House is one of Charlotte’s most celebrated independent bubble tea and Vietnamese cafe destinations, serving the Plaza Midwood and Central Avenue community since March 2019. Despite strong community acclaim, the shop lacks an authoritative owned website, leaving potential customers to navigate disjointed delivery platform listings, low-res social menu photos, and missing customization guidance. This 6-page concept provides a polished digital storefront celebrating their loose-leaf craft, scratch banh mi bakery, and Saigon Square location.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **In-House Loose-Leaf Tea Brewing:** 100% loose-leaf brewed teas (never instant powders) with tailored sweetness (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and handcrafted toppings (brown sugar boba, herbal grass jelly, silky egg pudding, salted cheese foam).
2. **Vietnamese Cafe Staples & Street Food:** Authentic slow-dripped Vietnamese iced coffee (Cà Phê Sữa Đá), crispy French-Vietnamese baguette banh mi sandwiches (lemongrass grilled pork, lemongrass chicken, tofu), and golden flaky patê sô (pâté chaud) meat pies.
3. **Saigon Square Community Anchor:** Located at 3000 Central Ave in East Charlotte’s historic multicultural district.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Artisanal Tea House Palette:** Deep Bamboo Matcha Green (`#255438`), Golden Honey Tapioca (`#d49228`), Crisp Jasmine Ivory (`#fdfbf7`), Saigon Brick Clay (`#b3402e`), and Espresso Slate (`#19211c`).
2. **Modern Tea Bar Architecture:** Clean split heroes, pill badges, drink customization matrices, and dual tea/kitchen showcase cards.
3. **Distinctive Editorial Typography:** Outfit / Syne modern geometric headings paired with Plus Jakarta Sans body and DM Mono for precision drink customizers.
