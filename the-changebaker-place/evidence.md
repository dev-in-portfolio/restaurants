## Original Audit Weakness

The café opened in March 2026 and its owned website is an unfinished WordPress stub: the homepage literally says "We are under cosntruction," the blog still carries the default WordPress sample comment and an untitled sample post, and there is no menu, no story, no meeting-room information, and no phone number anywhere — the reservation instructions say "please call us" without listing a number. A business whose entire identity is community mission currently cannot communicate that mission online. The demo shows what an owned hub looks like when the counter, the rooms, the story, and the neighborhood each get a real page.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **The civic living room** — Hoodline describes Betancur's own framing: "equal parts coffee shop and civic living room"; the space has laptop-friendly tables, cozy seating, a large communal dining table (visible in listing photos, echoed in reviews: "It was giving Grandma's house"), and small meeting rooms local groups can reserve.
2. **The documentary origin** — the café is named for and "inspired in the award winning documentary The Changebaker" (owned Instagram bio; Hoodline confirms the project "grew out of the short documentary").
3. **The bakery next door** — the café sits beside Manolo's Bakery on Central Ave and pours Colombian espresso alongside the bakery's pastries; reviews specifically name pastries from Manolo's, the Tres Leches Latte, and the Floral Chai Latte.
4. **The mission** — the space is a real-world extension of the nonprofit By Immigrant Hands, celebrating immigrant contributions in Charlotte.
5. **Central Ave, East Charlotte** — 4405 Central Ave Suite B, in a former hair salon, in the corridor where Betancur's next concepts (Artisen Gelato, Higher Grounds at Eastland Yards) are planned.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Editorial masthead instead of a hero banner** — the homepage opens with an oversized Fraunces serif headline set like a neighborhood gazette, a drop-cap lede, and a thin rule grid; type itself is the hero image. No full-bleed photo hero, no centered pill-button stack.
2. **Film-strip chapter motif** — the story page runs as numbered "reels" with sprocket-hole borders (a documentary reference), and section markers across the site use reel-frame numbering (REEL 01, REEL 02) instead of generic card grids.
3. **Counter-case menu composition** — the menu is organized by the room's actual physical cases (espresso bar, pastry case, gelato case, sandwich board) with dotted leaders and "case notes" in the owner's-voice margin, rather than a conventional two-column price list.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `the-brickyard` — dark soot/brick gig-poster collage, Anton marquee caps, rotated ticket stubs, ticker band.
2. `midtown-tavern` — copper-bronze industrial split hero, Outfit display, tavern card grids.
3. `mert-s-heart-and-soul` — Playfair Display Charleston-porch warmth, alternating two-column sections.
4. `medusa-lounge` — Cormorant Garamond obsidian-and-gold centered luxury hero.
5. `medellin-colombian-restaurant` — emerald hacienda editorial with large framed imagery.

### Dominant Pattern Being Avoided

Dark or saturated full-bleed hero → centered headline → 3-up card row → alternating image/text bands; heavy display sans or classic luxury serif; dark palettes.

### Divergent Choices for The ChangeBaker Place

- **Primary hero composition:** a light, typography-led editorial masthead (asymmetric two-column gazette front page with drop cap and rule grid) — the opposite weight and structure of the dark poster-collage and split-hero demos.
- **Section rhythm:** numbered reel-frame chapters and a bulletin-board strip replace card grids; the menu page is a case-by-case ledger, not a card row.
- **Typography system:** Fraunces (soft literary serif, optical sizing) + Work Sans — neither the Playfair/Cormorant luxury serifs nor the Anton/Outfit display sans of the five demos above.
- **Major page composition divergence:** `the-story.html` is a vertical film-reel timeline with sprocket borders; `menu.html` is a four-case counter ledger — two composition families that share nothing with the Brickyard's poster wall.

## Add-On Preservation

### Relevant DSC Add-On Opportunities

- **Meeting-Room Booking Engine (Order & Reserve / Catering & Private Events territory):** real-time room availability, rental rates, and reservation workflows.
- **Digital Menu Concierge / Menu Experience Pack:** rotating pastry-case and seasonal-drink menus with dietary filtering.
- **Newsletter/Community Calendar (Guest Engagement):** the mission audience is a natural email/community-calendar upsell.
- **Photo Story Pack:** the documentary origin and bakery-next-door story deserve a proper photo editorial.

### Intentionally Not Implemented (Preserved for Upsell)

- No reservation or booking system of any kind — room inquiries route to the verified email address only.
- No forms (the real site's WPForms contact form is deliberately not reproduced).
- No menu filtering, search, or pairing logic.
- No newsletter signup, event calendar system, or donation/nonprofit-fundraising machinery.

### Available for Production Scope

- Room-rental booking with rates and availability.
- Community events calendar tied to By Immigrant Hands programming.
- Bilingual (EN/ES) menu system with seasonal scheduling.
