## Original Audit Weakness

Golden Bakery is an East Charlotte institution, serving the Carolinas since 1981 with wood-hearth pita, authentic Aleppo flatbreads, and handcrafted pastries. However, their web presence was limited to directory stubs and delivery portal menus that omit the craftsmanship behind their hearth ovens, flour milling, halal market butcher counter, and holiday pastry catering. This 6-page concept provides an authoritative, elegant digital platform honoring their four-decade legacy.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Hearth-Baked Manakish & Fresh Pita:** Blistered brick-oven flatbreads topped with wild thyme za'atar and virgin olive oil, sweet and salty Akkawi cheese, spiced ground meat Lahm Bi'Ajeen, and daily puffy pita pockets.
2. **Savory Levantine Wraps & Pies:** Spit-roasted chicken and beef shawarma with house garlic toum, crispy herb falafel with nutty tahini, and golden baked spinach and feta fatayer pastries.
3. **Aleppo Pastry Tradition:** Layers of paper-thin filo dough brushed with pure clarified butter, stuffed with emerald green pistachios, and sweetened with light orange blossom syrup.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Hearth Terracotta & Pistachio Green Palette:** Hearth Terracotta (`#c85a17`), Pistachio Green (`#2b6e4f`), Warm Aleppo Spice Ochre (`#d97706`), and Sesame Cream (`#faf6ed`).
2. **Artisan Bakery & Market Visual Architecture:** Hearth temperature callouts, flatbread selection grids, and pastry dozen gift box showcases.
3. **Refined Mediterranean Typography:** Cinzel / Outfit display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear bilingual Arabic/English translations and pricing.
