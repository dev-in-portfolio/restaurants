## Original Audit Weakness

Tabla Indian Restaurant is South Charlotte’s premier dining destination for royal North and South Indian cuisine, located at 15025 Lancaster Highway in the Ballantyne corridor. Celebrated for clay tandoor oven roasting, slow-simmered Hyderabadi dum biryanis, velvety butter chicken, and crispy dosas, Tabla is an essential dining anchor for families and corporate professionals across South Charlotte and Fort Mill. Third-party aggregator scrapers frequently present confusing menus, mix up locations with unrelated operators, or fail to highlight the restaurant’s authentic clay-oven baking, scratch spice blending, and comprehensive Halal and vegetarian offerings.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Ballantyne / South Charlotte Location:** 15025 Lancaster Hwy, Ste D8, Charlotte, NC 28277.
2. **Weekly Operating Hours:** Daily 11:30 AM – 9:30 PM (Weekday Lunch Specials Mon–Fri 11:30 AM – 3:00 PM).
3. **Clay Tandoor Oven Mastery:** Burning intense heat to bake blistered garlic naans and sear yogurt-marinated chicken tikka kebabs and seekh kebabs.
4. **Royal Curry & Biryani Craft:** Velvety Butter Chicken, Chicken Tikka Masala, Paneer Makhani, slow-cooked Hyderabadi Dum Biryani with saffron basmati rice, and golden Punjabi samosas with mint chutney.
5. **Diverse Regional Menus:** North Indian curries, South Indian dosas, Halal certified meats, and Indo-Chinese specialties (Gobi Manchurian, Chicken 65).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Royal Saffron & Terracotta Palette:** Deep masala obsidian (`#141113`), terracotta slate (`#241b1f`), royal saffron gold (`#e69a28`), tandoori paprika red (`#c93c20`), and basmati cream (`#faf6f0`).
2. **Subcontinental Royal Typography:** Regal display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with warm utilitarian sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced spice station markers (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** Oval porcelain dish of velvety chicken tikka masala with blistered garlic naan; golden crisp Punjabi samosas with mint-coriander chutney; and a tandoori mixed grill platter with kebabs and fresh vegetables.
