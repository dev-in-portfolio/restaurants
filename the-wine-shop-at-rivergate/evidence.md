## Original Audit Weakness

The Wine Shop at Rivergate is Steele Creek’s premier culinary wine bar, boutique bottle cellar, and scratch European-American bistro, located at 14142 Rivergate Parkway, Suite 200 in Charlotte, NC. Featuring a vast library of over 500 hand-selected global wines alongside a dedicated culinary kitchen, The Wine Shop serves artisan charcuterie and cured meat boards with house focaccia, crispy prosciutto and arugula flatbreads, sesame-crusted pan-seared Ahi tuna, grilled Angus flatiron steaks, pressed Italian paninis, and sommelier-curated wine flights. Despite enduring local popularity, weekly live jazz nights, and a vibrant dog-friendly outdoor patio, basic online search engines frequently miscategorize the business as a standard package retail liquor store, completely obscuring its full dine-in bistro menu, table uncorking service, and weekend afternoon dining hours.
