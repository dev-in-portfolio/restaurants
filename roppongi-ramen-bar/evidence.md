## Original Audit Weakness

Roppongi Ramen Bar is one of Southeast Charlotte's premier destinations for authentic Japanese noodle craftsmanship and Korean comfort food, located at 9626 Monroe Road in the MoRA / Matthews corridor. While their in-person dining draws dedicated regulars for 18-hour simmered tonkotsu broths, classic Tokyo shoyu ramen, and double-fried soy garlic chicken, online directory listings flatten their offering into generic Asian takeout. The website must capture their artisanal noodle craft, deliberate broth simmering discipline, and vibrant ramen bar dining atmosphere.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Southeast Charlotte Corridor Flagship:** Located at 9626 Monroe Rd, Charlotte NC 28270. Phone: (980) 339-5353. Email: `charlie@roppongiramenbar.com`.
2. **18-Hour Simmered Broths & Artisan Noodles:** Slow-simmered rich Tonkotsu, Tokyo Shoyu with dashi, Clear Shio (salt, chicken, dried seafood), Spicy Miso, and rich Shiitake Vegan broths served with wavy alkaline wheat noodles, tender rolled chashu pork belly, and marinated ajitsuke tamago eggs.
3. **Double-Fried Korean Fried Chicken:** Extra crispy wings and drumsticks tossed in signature sweet soy garlic or spicy gochujang glaze, garnished with toasted sesame seeds and served with pickled daikon radish.
4. **Authentic Izakaya Starters:** Pan-fried pork gyoza dumplings with crispy golden bottoms, takoyaki octopus balls with bonito flakes, and chashu pork buns.
5. **Operating Schedule:** Mon: Closed, Tue: 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM, Wed–Thu: 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM & 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM, Fri: 11:30 AM – 2:30 PM & 4:30 PM – 9:30 PM, Sat: 11:30 AM – 9:30 PM, Sun: 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Tokyo Izakaya Ink & Vermilion Ramen Palette:** Deep lacquer obsidian (`#0e0d0c`), warm charred wood card (`#181513`), ramen vermilion red (`#d23b28`), bamboo gold (`#e5a93b`), and steaming broth ivory (`#fcfaf6`).
2. **Refined Japanese Modernist Typography:** High-contrast editorial display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced kitchen callouts (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Bowl & Plate Photography:** Sunlit artisan bowl of Tokyo Shoyu ramen with chashu pork, soy eggs, narutomaki, and menma on natural wood counter, crispy double-fried Korean chicken glazed in soy garlic, and golden pan-seared pork gyoza dumplings.
