## Original Audit Weakness

South End Hideaway is an anchor of Charlotte’s vibrant South End nightlife district, located at 332 W Bland St B directly off the Bland Street light rail corridor. Featuring two sprawling levels, two full bars, four pool tables, darts, rotating resident DJs, and an exclusive culinary partnership with the YUMBII food truck serving Asian-Mexican fusion tacos, it is one of Charlotte’s premier social destinations. However, third-party aggregator listings often fail to capture its multi-level versatility—treating it as either a generic dive or a standard dance club without highlighting its afternoon games, food truck residency, or craft beer flight program.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Bland Street Location:** Located at 332 W Bland St B, Charlotte NC 28203. Phone: `(704) 910-6710`.
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Closed Mondays; Tue–Thu: 4:00 PM – 12:00 AM; Fri: 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sat: 12:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sun: 12:00 PM – 12:00 AM.
3. **Two-Level Social Club & Two Full Bars:** Spanning an upper lounge and lower entertainment floor with two full bars, four regulation pool tables, and steel-tip darts.
4. **YUMBII Asian-Mexican Taco Residency:** Serving hot street tacos (sesame beef, spicy pulled pork, Asian ribeye) and loaded sesame fries late into the night.
5. **Nightlife & Daytime Versatility:** Transitioning seamlessly from sunny weekend sports hangouts and craft beer flights to vibrant late-night DJ sets.

### Core Design Moves

1. **South End Velvet Night & Neon Gold Palette:** Deep nightlife charcoal (`#0c0e12`), velvet lounge slate card (`#151921`), neon cyan accent (`#00f0ff`), burnished amber gold (`#f2a900`), and clean chalk white (`#f5f7fa`).
2. **Contemporary Social Club Typography:** Expressive serif (*Cinzel*) paired with modern grotesque sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced technical accents (*Space Mono*).
3. **High-Res Culinary & Entertainment Photography:** Three authentic street tacos on warm corn tortillas with cilantro and lime, crystalline sphere Old Fashioned cocktail, and an eight-pour craft beer tasting paddle on rich wood.
