## Original Audit Weakness

Salted Melon Market & Eatery is one of Charlotte’s most vibrant culinary concepts, seamlessly merging an everyday gourmet scratch kitchen with an artisanal market, specialty espresso bar, craft cocktail program, and dual-neighborhood presence (South End and Phillips Place in SouthPark). However, the existing digital representation does not showcase the dynamic multi-part dayparts—from morning specialty coffee and avocado toast to midday grain bowls and evening seated dinner and craft cocktails.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **South End Flagship & Phillips Place Outpost:** Flagship located at 100 W. Worthington Ave, Suite 107, Charlotte NC 28203 (South End Rail Trail corridor). Second outpost at 6809-A Phillips Place Ct, Charlotte NC 28210.
2. **Everyday Gourmet Scratch Repertoire:** Morning classics (Artisan Avocado Toast, The Notorious B.E.C. bagel, Bandito Burrito), fresh grain bowls, hand-crafted salads, prime smash burgers, specialty coffee and espresso bar, craft cocktails, and retail market provisions.
3. **Hours & Operations:** Phone (704) 817-9557; Monday – Friday: 7:00 AM – 8:30 PM, Saturday – Sunday: 8:00 AM – 8:30 PM. Seated dinner service begins at 4:00 PM; Sunday brunch at 11:00 AM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Melon Terracotta & Botanical Sage Palette:** Warm gourmet market palette featuring deep dark slate (`#111614`), botanical sage card (`#19241f`), ripe cantaloupe melon terracotta (`#d96b43`), crisp celery green (`#4e7a5d`), and fresh ivory linen (`#fcfbf8`).
2. **Contemporary Editorial Typography:** Clean modern serif (*Lora*) paired with contemporary geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced market pricing counters (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Gourmet Market Photography:** Vibrant market gnocchi buddha bowl with baby spinach, tomatoes, and pomodoro, sourdough avocado toast topped with poached farm eggs and shaved parmesan, and specialty swan latte art on saucer with confections.

### Divergent Choices for Salted Melon Market & Eatery

- **Hero Composition:** Modern neighborhood market split-hero featuring a South End Worthington Kicker (`"SOUTH END • 100 W WORTHINGTON AVE • CHARLOTTE NC"`), melon terracotta CTA buttons, and a framed hero dish of an artisanal market bowl, anchored by a floating hero badge (`South End Flagship | 100 W Worthington Ave • Everyday Gourmet`).
- **Section Rhythm:** Three-card standards grid without emojis highlighting "Everyday Scratch Kitchen", "Specialty Coffee & Espresso Bar", and "Curated Artisan Provisions", followed by dual alternating highlight banners.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Bespoke classes (`melon-header`, `melon-brand`, `melon-hero-stage`, `melon-hero-badge`, `melon-standards-section`, `melon-standard-card`, `melon-highlight-banner`, `melon-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
