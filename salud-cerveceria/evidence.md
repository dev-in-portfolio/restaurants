## Original Audit Weakness

Salud Cerveceria is one of Charlotte’s most celebrated national culinary institutions—a multi-time James Beard Foundation semifinalist and three-time USA Today 10Best #1 Beer Bar in America. Located at 3306-C North Davidson Street in the heart of NoDa, Salud combines an Afro-Latino and Latinx brewing ethos with house-brewed craft lagers, wild sours, a world-class bottle shop, crispy wood-fired bar-style pizzas, and morning espresso service. However, standard online listings underrepresent the depth of their wood-fired pizza kitchen, their house culinary culture, and their dual-level brewery taproom.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Heart of NoDa Arts District Flagship:** Located at 3306-C North Davidson Street, Charlotte NC 28205 in historic North Charlotte (NoDa). Phone: (980) 495-6612.
2. **Crispy Wood-Fired Bar-Style Pizzas:** Thin, blistered, cheesy pies including *Hootie Hoo Margherita*, *Latin Lingo* (spicy crema base, roasted corn, chicken, cotija), and *Return of the G* (white pie with roasted mushrooms and zucchini).
3. **Pioneering Brewery & USA Today #1 Beer Bar:** House-brewed lagers, hazy IPAs, wild fermented sours, curated international bottle shop, and craft beer tasting flights.
4. **Mamacita Meatballs & Sandwiches:** Braised beef and pork meatballs in house San Marzano marinara (*Mamacita Meatballs*), *Player's Ball* meatball sub with pesto, and crispy wings.
5. **Operating Hours:** Mon–Thu: 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM, Fri–Sat: 8:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight), Sun: 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Brewery Brass & Burnt Umber Taproom Palette:** Deep taproom noir (`#110f0d`), warm brewery brick card (`#1b1613`), brewery brass gold (`#df9b35`), burnt pizza crust umber (`#c44d2b`), and warm foam cream (`#fdfbf7`).
2. **Bold Industrial Arts District Typography:** Robust editorial serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced ABV/pricing tags (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Taproom & Kitchen Photography:** Crisp wood-fired thin bar-style pizza with charred edges and roasted mushrooms on a wooden table, four-pour craft beer tasting flight with creamy white foam heads, and rich braised Mamacita meatballs in tomato marinara with fresh basil.

## Add-On Preservation

- **NoDa Pizza & Growler Pickup Scheduler:** Interactive time-slot ordering for hot bar-style pizzas and fresh-filled crowlers or growlers.
