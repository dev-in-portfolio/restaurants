## Original Audit Weakness

Zafran Kabab Palace is a premier 100% Halal Pakistani, Indian, and Afghan culinary destination in Charlotte's University City corridor (230 E W.T. Harris Blvd, Suite C5/C6, Charlotte, NC 28262). Famous for live charcoal tandoori clay oven skewers (Seekh Kababs, Chapli Kabab, Beef Bihari, Lamb Chops), slow-simmered Beef Nihari, Mutton Dum Biryani ($16.15), and fresh clay oven garlic naan ($5.39), the restaurant operates a outdated WordPress/Kava theme installation with unstyled typography, slow image load overhead, missing standalone dedicated pages for its signature charcoal BBQ and weekend nihari specials, and unoptimized mobile ordering conversion.
