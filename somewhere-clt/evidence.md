## Original Audit Weakness

Somewhere CLT is an established nightlife and social club destination located at 2815 The Plaza in Charlotte’s vibrant Plaza Midwood corridor. Operating with an upscale 25+ age policy, craft cocktail mixology, indoor velvet lounge booths, an outdoor courtyard patio, premium shisha hookah service with natural coconut coals, and signature late-night kitchen specialties like smoked turkey ribs and hot honey glazed wings, it commands a devoted local following. However, third-party social listings frequently omit full operational hours, courtyard patio accommodations, private VIP section bookings, or full cocktail listings.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Plaza Midwood Location:** Located at 2815 The Plaza, Charlotte NC 28205. Phone: `(704) 465-5509`.
2. **Evening Operating Hours & 25+ Policy:** Monday through Friday: 7:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Saturday and Sunday: 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM. 25+ age requirement for an elevated adult lounge environment.
3. **Craft Cocktail & Bottle Service Lineup:** Top-shelf spirits, handcrafted signature cocktails like the Plaza Blossom Martini, Hennessy highballs, Casamigos infusions, and VIP champagne presentations.
4. **Premium Hookah Craft:** Multi-hose shisha service prepared with precision coconut charcoals, custom citrus heads, and premium tobacco blends.
5. **Late-Night Kitchen Staples:** Smoked Turkey Ribs, Hot Honey Garlic Barbecue Wings with celery and house dipping sauce, artisanal flatbreads, and seasoned fries.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Plaza Noir & Violet Velvet Palette:** Deep night obsidian (`#0c0b11`), plush royal plum card (`#171422`), radiant magenta neon (`#e02e86`), electric violet (`#8b5cf6`), and chilled platinum white (`#f5f3ff`).
2. **Contemporary Luxury Typography:** High-contrast display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with sleek modern sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced tech accents (*Space Mono*).
3. **Authentic Nightlife & Culinary Photography:** Elegant orchid-garnished craft martini on granite table, glowing coconut charcoals arranged over premium hookah bowl, and glazed barbecue tavern wings with fresh celery and dressing.
