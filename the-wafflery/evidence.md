## Original Audit Weakness

The Wafflery is Charlotte's premier artisanal Belgian waffle, stone-ground grit bowl, and Southern breakfast house, headquartered at 1020 East 10th Street, Suite 3 in Charlotte's Belmont/First Ward neighborhood with expansion hubs on Rozzelles Ferry Road and Docia Crossing Road. Blending deep-pocket golden Belgian waffle craftsmanship with heirloom Carolina breakfast traditions, The Wafflery serves crispy buttermilk Chicken and Waffles, cinnamon-spiced Peaches and Cream waffles, rich stone-ground Shrimp and Grits bowls, fiery hot honey chicken biscuits, and slow-steeped artisan cold brew. Despite high neighborhood acclaim and thriving morning queues, third-party delivery aggregators often portray The Wafflery as a generic fast-food diner, omitting its scratch waffle batter recipes, extensive vegan breakfast accommodations, Carolina grit bowl program, and flagship morning cafe atmosphere.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Flagship Location:** 1020 E 10th St, Suite 3, Charlotte, NC 28204 (10th Street Flagship).
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Monday – Friday 7:00 AM – 2:00 PM; Saturday – Sunday 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM.
3. **Belgian & Southern Breakfast Craft:** Chicken & Waffles with spiced syrup, Peaches & Cream waffle with vanilla whipped cream, Banana Caramel Crunch, Red Velvet, and vegan waffle options.
4. **Carolina Stone-Ground Grit Bowls:** Signature Shrimp & Grits with andouille gravy and bacon, Crabcake Grit Bowls, and Fried Chicken Grit Bowls.
5. **Morning Beverage Program:** 18-hour cold brew coffee, Southern peach sweet tea, and fresh-squeezed strawberry lemonade.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Warm Honeycomb & Morning Roast Palette:** Deep espresso charcoal (`#181513`), warm golden maple (`#d48b38`), waffle honeycomb (`#f2b84b`), peach cream (`#fdf4ea`), biscuit beige (`#e6d6be`), and warm roast brown (`#2c221b`).
2. **Artisan Breakfast Typography:** Warm editorial serif (*Fraunces*) paired with friendly geometric sans (*Outfit*) and crisp morning cafe monospace (*IBM Plex Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A deep-pocket golden Belgian waffle topped with crispy fried chicken and served with warm peaches and whipped cream; a creamy Carolina cheese grit bowl loaded with sautéed shrimp, andouille gravy, and scallions; and two condensation-beaded glasses of artisan iced cold brew coffee on a morning cafe patio.
