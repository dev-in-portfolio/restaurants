## Original Audit Weakness

The Craic opened in April 2026 with a strong concept and a Popmenu template that buries the story: the menu renders as an endless JS feed, the "craic" name — the entire brand idea — is never explained on the site itself, and the private-dining rooms, the 31-tap bar, and the all-day brunch-to-2AM arc are scattered across separate app screens. The audit weakness is an identity gap: a name that needs a translation and a story that needs a home. The demo makes the definition the hero and gives each verified asset (taps, brunch, rooms, neighborhood) a real page.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **The word itself** — "Craic" is a Gaelic word for lively conversation, good times, and the feeling of connection (owned Yelp specialties text). A dictionary-entry presentation is the brand's own story.
2. **31 taps** — the bar program's headline number (Charlotte Business Journal), plus recurring Personalized Guinness Pint Nights on the SouthPark calendar (Aug 27 & Sep 17, 2026).
3. **The all-day arc** — daily brunch through late night, 11 AM to 2 AM every day: brunch, oven-fired pizzas, burgers, wings, Irish stew, fish and chips (Axios), with salmon pasta, charcuterie, and sweet potato fries as guest favorites (Restaurantji).
4. **Two private dining areas** — seated dinners and cocktail-style events in Sharon Square (SouthPark directory).
5. **The pedigree** — opened April 15, 2026 by "a seasoned team of Charlotte hospitality leaders" — the same team behind The Public House in Uptown — in the former Rusty Bucket space.

### Core Design Moves

1. **The dictionary hero** — the homepage opens with the word set as a dictionary entry: "craic (krak) · noun — lively conversation, good times, the feeling of connection." The brand's own definition becomes the masthead; no photo hero, no banner.
2. **Vertical session rail** — a slim plum rail with rotated session text ("BRUNCH → LATE NIGHT • 11 TO 2 • SOUTHPARK") anchors the left edge on desktop, turning the all-day arc into structure rather than a slogan; pages share the rail as a wayfinding spine.
3. **Knot-line dividers & tap-number ledger** — a single-line Celtic knot motif drawn as an inline SVG divider between sections, and the pints page presented as a numbered tap ledger (01–31) instead of card grids.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `the-corner-pub` — green/cream/brass scoreboard panels, Oswald + Zilla Slab, crossroads diagram, fixture cards.
2. `the-brickyard` — dark poster collage, Anton, ticket stubs, ticker.
3. `the-changebaker-place` — cream gazette, Fraunces, drop caps, film reels.
4. `midtown-tavern` — copper split hero, Outfit, card grids.
5. `mert-s-heart-and-soul` — Playfair porch warmth, alternating bands.

### Dominant Pattern Being Avoided

Hero banner → centered headline → 3-up cards → alternating bands; scoreboard panels; poster collage; gazette columns.

### Divergent Choices for The Craic

- **Primary hero composition:** a typographic dictionary entry with pronunciation and part of speech, set against deep plum with a vertical session rail — structurally unlike the panel, poster, gazette, and scoreboard heroes above.
- **Section rhythm:** knot-line SVG dividers and a numbered tap ledger replace card grids and boards.
- **Typography system:** Young Serif (warm modern bookish serif) + Archivo — a pairing unused by any recent demo, deliberately not the Playfair/Cormorant luxury register nor condensed-sport registers.
- **Major page composition divergence:** `pints.html` is a 31-row tap ledger; `gather.html` is a two-room spread with a diagram — distinct composition families within one site.

## Add-On Preservation

### Relevant DSC Add-On Opportunities

- **Catering & Private Events Pack:** room diagrams, capacity charts, and inquiry workflows for the two private dining areas.
- **Digital Menu Concierge / Menu Experience Pack:** the JS-feed menu could become a fast structured menu with photos and dietary filters.
- **Guest Engagement Pack:** the site's "Win $500 review" promo and "Stay In the Know" block point to engagement machinery worth building properly.
- **Events Calendar Pack (Happenings):** Guinness Pint Nights and recurring programming deserve a real calendar system.

### Intentionally Not Implemented (Preserved for Upsell)

- No reservation, waitlist, or booking logic — reservations route to the pub's existing SevenRooms link.
- No forms; private-event interest routes to verified contact paths.
- No menu search/filtering, tap filters, or pairing engines — the tap ledger is static.
- No newsletter/SMS capture, loyalty, or review-automation machinery.

### Available for Production Scope

- Private-events package builder with room selection.
- Tap-list management system with live availability.
- Happenings calendar with reminders.
