## Original Audit Weakness

Three Amigos Mexican Grill & Cantina is an enduring Central Avenue institution founded in 2010 at 2917 Central Avenue, Suite A, in Charlotte’s Plaza Midwood culinary corridor. Rooted in traditional interior Mexican gastronomy, Three Amigos specializes in char-grilled Carne Asada with blistered cebollitas, slow-simmered Bistec a la Mexicana, hand-rolled Enchiladas Suizas in creamy tomatillo sauce, velvety Mole Poblano de la Casa, tacos al pastor with grilled pineapple, and freshly shaken blue agave margaritas. Despite being celebrated by Charlotte culinary critics as one of the Queen City's most authentic Mexican neighborhood destinations, basic search aggregators often confuse it with generic Tex-Mex chains or display outdated lunch opening times, omitting its full scratch kitchen recipes, expansive cantina tequila collection, and weekend all-day service.
