## Original Audit Weakness

The Hobbyist is Villa Heights' premier hybrid coffee shop, craft bottle shop, and cocktail lounge, located at 2100 North Davidson Street, Suite D, along the vibrant NoDa corridor. Celebrating an effortless transition from morning remote-work cafe to lively evening neighborhood lounge, The Hobbyist pairs specialty espresso roasts from Hatchet Coffee (Boone, NC) with 14 rotating draft beer taps, an extensive curated cooler of regional craft beers and natural wines, and handcrafted cocktails headlined by its renowned velvety Espresso Martinis. Third-party aggregator listings frequently flatten the concept into either a simple drive-thru coffee stand or a basic tavern, completely missing its dual day-to-night character, bottle shop retail privileges, laptop-friendly workspace hospitality, and community tasting events.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Villa Heights / NoDa Corridor Location:** 2100 N Davidson St, Suite D, Charlotte, NC 28205.
2. **Day-to-Night Operating Schedule:** Mon–Thu 7:30 AM – 9:00 PM; Fri 7:30 AM – 10:00 PM; Sat 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM; Sun 8:30 AM – 6:00 PM.
3. **Specialty Morning Coffee & House Syrups:** Precision espresso drinks pulled with Hatchet Coffee beans, including the signature Black Walnut Latte, Lavender Vanilla Cortado, and organic loose-leaf teas paired with local pastries.
4. **Curated Craft Beer & Bottle Shop:** 14 rotating craft draft beers available as pints or flights, alongside refrigerated retail coolers stocked with rare IPAs, sours, and natural wines to enjoy on premise or take to-go.
5. **Evening Craft Cocktails & Espresso Martinis:** Handcrafted cocktail program featuring rich, frothy Espresso Martinis pulled with fresh house espresso, alongside seasonal spritzes, bourbon pours, and Italian amari.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Botanical Industrial & Warm Espresso Palette:** Espresso bean obsidian (`#141110`), roaster slate (`#211d1b`), warm walnut wood (`#2c2420`), botanical sage (`#4d685a`), crema amber gold (`#d89b43`), and warm oat milk cream (`#f8f5f0`).
2. **Contemporary Cafe-Lounge Typography:** Editorial modern serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean legible modern body (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced barista/draft indexes (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A terracotta ceramic cup of artisanal cappuccino with intricate rosetta latte art on a rustic wood cafe table; a live-edge wooden beer flight paddle holding eight glasses of craft ales across a mahogany bar; and a velvety Espresso Martini with thick crema foam on a polished bar in front of spirit bottles.
