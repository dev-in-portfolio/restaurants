## Original Audit Weakness

Littl Madeleine relies on basic Shopify/Wix web templates and social media posts. Located in upscale Ballantyne on N Community House Rd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its "less-sweet" organic Korean pastry philosophy, seasonal madeleine flavor drops, luxury gift box pre-orders, and cafe seating directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Ballantyne Charlotte Location:** 11318 N Community House Rd, #202, Charlotte NC 28277 (Ballantyne).
2. **Boutique Bakery Specialties:** Signature Organic Madeleines (Earl Grey Glazed, Blueberry Lemon, Cinnamon Churro, Double Chocolate), Basque Burnt Cheesecake, Japanese/Korean Fluffy Egg Sandos, Roasted Misugaru Multi-Grain Lattes, and Organic Espresso.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Email `littlmadeleine@gmail.com`; Mon–Fri 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM | Sat 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM | Closed Sundays.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Delicate Parisian Display Serif Typography:** High-fashion serif (*Italiana* / *Cormorant Garamond*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and Seoul boutique ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Rose Blush & Champagne Gold Palette:** Feminine haute pâtisserie palette anchored in soft rose petal blush (`#FFF1F2`), deep plum velvet (`#4C0519`), warm French vanilla cream (`#FEF3C7`), golden honey (`#D97706`), and clean linen white.
3. **"The Organic Madeleine & Basque Cheesecake Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`organic-madeleine-and-pastry-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing organic madeleine collections and Basque cheesecakes alongside savory fluffy egg sandos and Korean misugaru grain lattes.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Divergent Choices for Littl Madeleine

- **Hero Composition:** Korean-French Haute Pâtisserie & Madeleine Salon split-hero layout: left side features an Organic Madeleine Seal badge (`"KOREAN-FRENCH ORGANIC MADELEINE BOUTIQUE • BALLANTYNE CHARLOTTE"`), delicate Parisian serif typography (*Italiana*), rose blush & plum velvet palette, and right side features a framed hero image of golden glazed madeleines and delicate French pastries.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Madeleine Vault Cards** (`madeleine-vault-card`) and Ballantyne boutique bakery highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`madeleine-header`, `madeleine-brand`, `patisserie-hero-stage`, `madeleine-seal-badge`, `madeleine-vault-card`, `madeleine-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
