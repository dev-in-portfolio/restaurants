## Original Audit Weakness

Tryon House Restaurant — Woodlawn is a multi-generational Charlotte dining institution located at 215 E Exmore Street, just off South Boulevard in Charlotte’s Woodlawn / Lower South End (LoSo) corridor. Renowned for its all-day Southern scratch breakfasts, char-broiled Ribeye steak dinners, chopped hamburger steaks with caramelized sweet onions and gravy, authentic Greek gyro platters, and fresh-baked Southern dessert pies, Tryon House has served generations of Queen City locals. However, online mapping algorithms often conflate this operating Woodlawn diner on Exmore Street with a previously closed location on Eastway Drive, causing confusion regarding active operating hours, breakfast service, and active dinner hours.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Physical Address:** 215 E Exmore St, Charlotte, NC 28217 (LoSo / Woodlawn corridor).
2. **Contact Phone:** (704) 527-6545.
3. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Tuesday – Saturday 6:30 AM – 8:00 PM; Sunday 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM; Closed Monday.
4. **All-Day Southern Breakfast:** Two-egg platters with crisp thick bacon, golden skillet hash browns, fluffy buttermilk pancake stacks, Greek feta omelettes, and hot buttermilk biscuits.
5. **Diner Dinners & Scratch Bakery:** Char-broiled ribeye steak dinners with loaded baked potatoes, chopped steak with Vidalia onions, gyro platters with house tzatziki, and scratch Carolina pecan pie.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Classic Diner Cream & Heritage Forest Palette:** Deep diner ironstone (`#151816`), warm heritage forest green (`#234b38`), rich griddle amber (`#c67d26`), buttermilk cream (`#f9f6ef`), warm linen (`#eae4d5`), and polished diner chrome (`#e2e8f0`).
2. **Enduring Family Diner Typography:** Warm traditional serif (*Playfair Display*) paired with friendly legible sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and diner ticket monospace (*DM Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A classic diner breakfast platter with sunny-side up eggs, thick crispy bacon, and golden hash browns with coffee and salt & pepper shakers; a massive bone-in char-broiled ribeye steak with a loaded sea-salt crusted baked potato; and a golden scratch-baked Southern pecan pie in a fluted pastry crust.
