## Original Audit Weakness

Night Swim Coffee relies on generic Shopify store templates and multi-brand sub-pages. Located at 4500 Old Pineville Rd in LoSo, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its single-origin sourcing ethics, roasting science, café barista craftsmanship, and Charlotte roastery directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Flagship Roastery & Charlotte Cafés:** 4500 Old Pineville Rd, Charlotte NC 28217 (Flagship Roastery in Lower South End / LoSo); additional cafes at 2161 Hawkins St (South End) and 620 S Tryon St (Uptown).
2. **Specialty Coffee Specialties:** Micro-Lot Direct-Trade Single-Origin Pour-Overs, Seasonal Signature Lattes (Cardamom Vanilla, Citrus Espresso Tonic), Nitrogen Flash Brews, Scratch Artisan Pastries & Sourdough Toast.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 332-2227; Mon–Fri 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM | Sat–Sun 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM (Flagship Roastery).

### Core Design Moves

1. **Nordic Architectural Typography:** Contemporary Nordic geometric sans (*Epilogue*) paired with clean body sans (*Inter*) and roaster batch mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Oceanic Midnight Navy & Wave Cyan Palette:** Minimalist oceanic palette anchored in deep oceanic navy (`#0B192C`), vibrant wave cyan (`#00A9FF`), electric cerulean (`#0284C7`), and crisp cloud slate (`#F8FAFC`).
3. **"The Micro-Lot Sourcing & Roastery Science Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`single-origin-roasting-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing direct-trade seasonal single-origin beans alongside espresso mixology & scratch breakfast toasts.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `Nalan Indian Cuisine` — Royal Indian Spice Palace with Marcellus fonts and Royal Terracotta/Turmeric Amber.
2. `Must Be Nice` — Glamorous South End Neon Velvet Nightclub with Syne fonts and Velvet Plum/Neon Magenta.
3. `Mother of Dragons` — Mythic Fantasy Tavern with Cinzel Decorative fonts and Obsidian Indigo/Ember Orange.

### Divergent Choices for Night Swim Coffee

- **Hero Composition:** Modern Nordic Minimalist Roastery & Deep Oceanic Swim Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Oceanic Coffee Stamp badge (`"DIRECT-TRADE SPECIALTY COFFEE ROASTERY • CHARLOTTE NC"`), architectural geometric display sans (*Epilogue*), deep oceanic midnight navy & wave cyan palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of signature iced latte craft.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Night Swim Vault Cards** (`swim-vault-card`) and Charlotte specialty coffee roasting highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`swim-header`, `swim-brand`, `ocean-hero-stage`, `swim-seal-badge`, `swim-vault-card`, `swim-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
