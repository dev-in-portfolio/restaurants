## Original Audit Weakness

Southern Range Brewing is the pioneering independent craft brewery and community hub of historic downtown Monroe, NC (151 S Stewart St in the greater Charlotte metropolitan area). Renowned for its award-winning hazy IPAs (*Tiny Ass Umbrella*, *Ice Cube's A Pimp*), refreshing fruited sours, $8 custom tasting flights, lively outdoor live music stage with bright red umbrellas, rotating local food trucks, and family/dog-friendly atmosphere, its current website lacks rich taproom storytelling, interactive event scheduling, and full mobile optimization.
