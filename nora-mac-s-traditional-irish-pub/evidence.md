## Original Audit Weakness

Nora Mac's relies on basic third-party online ordering portals and minimal web templates. Located at 11318 N Community House Rd in Ballantyne, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its authentic Dublin public house heritage, slow-cooked Irish comfort classics, master draft pour standards, and Ballantyne pub directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Ballantyne South Charlotte Location:** 11318 N Community House Rd #200, Charlotte NC 28277 (Ballantyne / South Charlotte).
2. **Authentic Celtic Pub Specialties:** Guinness-Braised Lamb Shepherd's Pie, Crisp Beer-Battered Wild Atlantic Cod Fish & Chips, Kilkenny Bangers & Colcannon Mash, Corned Beef & Swiss Boxty, 20oz Imperial Guinness Pours, & Single Pot Still Irish Whiskeys.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 595-3438; Mon–Sat 11:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight) | Sun 11:00 AM – 11:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Majestic Old-World Celtic Display Typography:** Stately Old-World Celtic display serif (*Cinzel Decorative* / *Cinzel*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and tavern draught ledger mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Dublin Stout Onyx & Celtic Shamrock Palette:** Authentic Irish tavern palette anchored in deep Dublin stout onyx (`#120B06`), Celtic shamrock emerald (`#047857`), antique amber brass (`#D97706`), and warm Irish linen (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Traditional Irish Comforts & Draught Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`irish-comfort-fare-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing hearty slow-simmered Dublin staples alongside master draught beers and Irish whiskeys.

## Cross-Demo Diversity

### Inspected Recent Demos

1. `NoDa Bodega` — Bohemian Arts Deli with Bungee fonts and Forest Olive/Mustard Relish.
2. `No Proof` — Modern Luxe Speakeasy with Prata fonts and Obsidian Onyx/Botanical Emerald.
3. `Ninety’s Dessert Bar` — Radical 90s Pop Retro Memphis with Righteous fonts and Memphis Teal/Neon Pink.

### Divergent Choices for Nora Mac's

- **Hero Composition:** Warm Dublin Public House & Celtic Mahogany Tavern Vault split-hero layout: left side features an Emerald Celtic Tavern Stamp badge (`"AUTHENTIC IRISH PUBLIC HOUSE & DRAUGHT VAULT • BALLANTYNE CHARLOTTE"`), stately Celtic display typography (*Cinzel Decorative*), deep stout black & shamrock emerald palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of golden pub classics and roaring hearth tavern hospitality.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Nora Mac's Vault Cards** (`celtic-vault-card`) and Charlotte Ballantyne community highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`celtic-header`, `celtic-brand`, `pub-hero-stage`, `celtic-seal-badge`, `celtic-vault-card`, `celtic-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
