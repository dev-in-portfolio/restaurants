## Original Audit Weakness

QC Social Lounge operates with a minimal landing page focused on ticket links and basic calendar dates. As Uptown Charlotte's preeminent boutique Latin nightclub and VIP bottle service sanctuary, it lacked an owned digital showcase conveying its high-design midnight aesthetic, concert-grade acoustic engineering, white-glove sparkler bottle presentations, and clear alleyway entrance directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Uptown Charlotte Location & Alley Entrance:** 300 N College St, Suite 105, Charlotte NC 28202. Discreet historic alleyway entrance between 6th and 7th Street.
2. **Nightlife & Beverage Program:** Handcrafted tequila & mezcal cocktails (Queen City Paloma, Midnight in Medellin, Obsidian Smoked Old Fashioned), prestige champagnes (Dom Pérignon, Ace of Spades, Veuve Clicquot, Moët Nectar Rosé), and luxury spirit bottles (Clase Azul Reposado, Don Julio 1942, Casamigos, Grey Goose, Hennessy XO).
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (980) 226-5115; Thu–Sat: 10:00 PM – 2:00 AM; Sun–Wed: Closed (Available for Private Buyouts and Corporate Events). Strictly 21+ with government photo ID.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Midnight Obsidian & Neon Velvet Palette:** Deep nocturnal luxury anchored in obsidian dark (`#07030c`), velvet plum (`#130a21`), neon electric orchid (`#c084fc`), and warm champagne amber gold (`#f59e0b`).
2. **High-Design Typography:** Clean geometric sans typography with high-contrast uppercase display hierarchy and technical monospace metadata tags.
3. **Bespoke Nightclub Photography:** High-energy packed dance floor with moving stage lights, chilled champagne bottle in illuminated ice bucket with crystal coupes, and hand-carved crystal ice craft cocktails on dark marble.
