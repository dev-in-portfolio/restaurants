## Original Audit Weakness

Sweet Lew’s BBQ is widely hailed as Charlotte’s premier wood-fired barbecue smokehouse, located at 923 Belmont Avenue in the historic Belmont neighborhood. Founded by Pitmaster Lewis Donald, Sweet Lew’s preserves genuine North Carolina wood-cooked barbecue tradition, burning logs down to coals in outdoor burn barrels and shoveling live coals under pork shoulders, beef briskets, and ribs for hours. Generic online scrapers often present it as a standard fast-food chain or generic diner, overlooking its wood-only smokehouse craftsmanship, Eastern vs. Western NC heritage sauces, hand-crafted sides, and daily scratch operations.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Belmont Neighborhood Smokehouse:** 923 Belmont Ave, Charlotte, NC 28205.
2. **Weekly Operating Hours:** Monday – Sunday: 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Open seven days a week).
3. **True Hardwood Pit Smoking:** Whole pork shoulders, beef brisket, and spare ribs smoked over hickory, white oak, and pecan hardwood coals with zero gas or electric assist.
4. **Carolina BBQ Specialties:** Hand-chopped NC pork shoulder with Eastern vinegar dip and Lexington red sauce, 15-hour Texas-style peppered beef brisket, St. Louis ribs, and smoked turkey.
5. **Southern Scratch Sides:** Baked mac & cheese, slow-simmered collard greens, southern potato salad, fresh sweet corn hushpuppies with honey butter, and banana pudding.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Belmont Smokehouse Charcoal & Ember Palette:** Pit charcoal obsidian (`#141312`), weathered pithouse timber (`#24211e`), smoldering hickory ember (`#c94c24`), barbecue golden honey (`#e29d38`), and butcher paper cream (`#faf6ed`).
2. **Authentic Southern Pitmaster Typography:** Heritage display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with rugged utilitarian sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced smokehouse cut tags (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Smokehouse Photography:** Metal cafeteria tray packed with sliced brisket, spare ribs, pulled pork, and baked beans; NC chopped pork sandwich piled high with crisp coleslaw; and barbecue spare ribs with home-style potato salad.
