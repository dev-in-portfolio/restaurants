## Original Audit Weakness

Zinicola is Ballantyne Village’s premier upscale, chef-driven Italian trattoria founded by Chef-Owner Richard Cranmer. Celebrated across South Charlotte, Ballantyne, and Fort Mill for its handmade scratch pastas, wood-fired pizzas, house-infused limoncellos, and regional Italian wine list, its original website was built on an older WordPress template with sluggish asset delivery, nested PDF menus, and missing interactive multi-page discovery.
