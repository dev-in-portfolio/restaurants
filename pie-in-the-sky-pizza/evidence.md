## Original Audit Weakness

Pie in the Sky Pizza is an essential piece of Uptown Charlotte's historic Brevard Court corridor, serving office workers, sports fans heading to Truist Field and Bank of America Stadium, and evening patio crowds. However, their online footprint was fragmented across basic directory listings without an interactive specialty pie builder, gameday slice availability guide, or group party catering details. This 6-page concept provides an authentic, energetic digital storefront for the pizzeria.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **New York-Style Hand-Tossed Pies & Jumbo Slices:** Foldable thin-crust dough baked on stone deck ovens with San Marzano tomato sauce, whole milk mozzarella, and blistered edges.
2. **Stuffed Calzones & Hot Oven Grinders:** Golden baked pizza dough pockets stuffed with creamy ricotta and mozzarella, paired with toasted meatball parmesan and Italian sausage heroes.
3. **Historic Brevard Court Courtyard Patio:** European-style cobblestone pedestrian alleyway seating under cafe string lights, pouring local Charlotte craft taps.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Brevard Brick Red & Cobblestone Charcoal Palette:** Brevard Brick Red (`#991b1b`), Cobblestone Charcoal (`#18181b`), Oven Crust Gold (`#f59e0b`), and Parchment Cream (`#fbf8f3`).
2. **Pizzeria Visual Architecture:** 18-inch pie sizing breakdowns, slice-by-the-day specials, and gameday stadium takeout alerts.
3. **Bold Modern Typography:** Outfit display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear whole pie and slice rates.
