The Art of the Neighborhood Dive

In a city filled with polished rooftops and cocktail lounges, Midwood Country Club remains fiercely committed to what a bar should be: cold beer in honest glasses, good jukebox music, familiar faces behind the bar, and zero pretension.

The Dive Philosophy

The 3 Pillars of Midwood Country Club

1. Honest, Affordable Pours

Keeping pints and shots priced fairly so working locals, service industry friends, and neighbors can unwind every day of the week.

2. Plaza Midwood Community Spirit

An inclusive gathering space where everyone is treated like a regular from the very first pint you order.

3. Classic Bar Games & Good Music

Felt pool tables, regulation dart boards, and an eclectic jukebox keeping the atmosphere lively late into the night.
