## Original Audit Weakness

Taqueria Los Altos is a beloved East Charlotte culinary cornerstone located at 8625 Monroe Road, Suite D. Known throughout the Monroe Road corridor for its authentic Jalisco / Los Altos regional cooking, the taqueria specializes in freshly griddled corn tortilla street tacos loaded with slow-braised carnitas, sizzling plancha carne asada with charred cebollitas, tangy tomatillo enchiladas verdes, and rich house-made salsas. Generic web directories frequently collapse Taqueria Los Altos into a boilerplate American-Mexican fast food listing, omitting its deep Jalisco culinary heritage, scratch plancha techniques, authentic weekend menudo hours, and expansive Monroe Road neighborhood loyalty.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Monroe Road Corridor Location:** 8625 Monroe Rd, Ste D, Charlotte, NC 28212 (near the Matthews / East Charlotte line).
2. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Mon–Wed 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM, Thu 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM (early afternoon closing), Fri 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM, Sat–Sun 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM (early weekend opening for authentic Mexican breakfast & soups).
3. **Double Corn Tortilla Street Tacos:** Freshly warmed corn tortillas heaped with tender carnitas, seared carne asada, al pastor, chorizo, or pollo asado, topped with fresh cilantro, diced white onion, and fresh lime wedges.
4. **Plancha Grill Specialties:** Traditional Carne Asada platters with charred whole cebollitas (green onions), Mexican red rice, slow-simmered refried beans, and fresh avocado; Chori Pollo; and sizzling Fajitas Mixtas.
5. **Authentic Platillos & Scratch Salsas:** Enchiladas Verdes bathed in tangy roasted tomatillo sauce, cotija cheese, shredded lettuce, and crema; complemented by roasted chile de árbol salsa roja and fresh pico de gallo.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Agave Plancha & Clay Terracotta Palette:** Earthy Jalisco obsidian (`#141210`), clay terracotta (`#241915`), agave green (`#2e6b48`), sun-baked chile red (`#b83324`), warm maize gold (`#e5a93b`), and masa crema (`#fbf8f2`).
2. **Authentic Taqueria Typography:** Stately Mexican heritage display serif (*Cinzel*) paired with clean legible modern body (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and monospaced kitchen tickets (*Space Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** An authentic green platter of street tacos on double corn tortillas with cilantro, onion, lime, and roasted salsas; a plancha-seared Carne Asada steak platter with charred cebollitas, rice, beans, and avocado; and fresh Enchiladas Verdes with salsa verde, cotija, and sliced avocado.
