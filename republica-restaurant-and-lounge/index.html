Island Comfort Heritage

Crispy Tostones con Salami Frito & Queso

Nothing defines Dominican social dining quite like hot, twice-fried green plantain tostones. Hand-pressed and fried until golden and shatteringly crisp, we serve them alongside seared Dominican salami frito, fried Caribbean queso blanco, sliced ripe avocado, and tangy house-blended mayo-ketchup.
