Age & Admission

Guest Policies

República welcomes guests of all ages for daytime lunch and evening family dinner service. After 10:00 PM on Friday and Saturday, entry requires guests to be 21+ with a valid government photo ID. Dress code is upscale casual.

Transit & Parking

Stress-Free Dedicated Surface Parking

Arriving at República is straightforward and convenient:

  • Expansive Free Lot: Ample, well-lit surface parking located directly surrounding our building. No parking decks, paid meters, or validation required.
  • Easy Highway Access: Conveniently accessible from US-74 E Independence Blvd, I-485 outer loop, and Matthews Township Parkway.
  • Rideshare Friendly: Easy pull-up curb area for Uber and Lyft drop-offs directly at our main entrance.
Nightlife Directives

Weekend Latin Dance & VIP Guidelines

To ensure a safe and celebratory environment for all patrons:

  • Dress Code: Smart casual and upscale nightlife attire encouraged. Athletic shorts, plain gym wear, and work boots prohibited after 10:00 PM.
  • Identification: Unexpired physical government photo ID required for late-night entry.
  • VIP Table Bookings: Advance reservations recommended for weekend bottle service parties. Inquire at (704) 246-8073.
