Dominican Cultural Identity

Two Worlds, One Vibrant Home.

República was created to fill an essential cultural space in the Charlotte metropolitan area. Hospitality Ethos

The Four Pillars of República

Guided by family warmth, culinary authenticity, and the joyful rhythm of Dominican culture.

Pillar 01

Authentic Heritage Cooking

We make no compromises in our kitchen. From fresh recao sofrito and long-simmered sancocho to hand-pressed tostones, our recipes honor the grandmothers and cocineras of Santo Domingo, Santiago, and Boca Chica.

Pillar 02

Generous Island Warmth

In the Dominican Republic, guests are received with an open heart, a plate of food, and an ice-cold drink. At República, every guest—whether visiting for a weekday lunch or a Saturday bottle service celebration—is treated like family.

Pillar 03

Community Celebration

Music is in the Dominican DNA. We provide a clean, safe, and exhilarating environment where our community comes together to dance, celebrate milestones, and share pride in Caribbean heritage.

Matthews Cultural Destination

A Beacon for Latin Excellence

Situated on East Independence Boulevard, República represents the expanding cultural diversity of the Charlotte-Matthews corridor. We welcome neighbors from every background to savor our flavors, enjoy our hospitality, and feel the infectious warmth of our island home.
