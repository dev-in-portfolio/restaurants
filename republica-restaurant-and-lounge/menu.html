Platos Fuertes • Traditional Dominican Entrées

Served with your choice of seasoned white rice, moro de habichuelas (rice & beans), tostones, or sweet plantains.

La Bandera Dominicana $18

The national dish of the Dominican Republic. Slow-braised tender chicken (pollo guisado) or beef (carne guisada) in rich tomato sofrito, fluffy white rice, stewed red kidney beans (habichuelas guisadas), and fried sweet plantains.

Sancocho Dominicano de Res y Víveres $22

Hearty traditional stew of bone-in beef shank, pork belly, corn on the cob, yuca, green plantains, and kabocha pumpkin simmered for four hours with fresh cilantro, garlic, and Dominican oregano. Served with white rice and avocado.

Churrasco a la Parrilla con Chimichurri $26

Char-grilled 10oz prime skirt steak marinated in island citrus and garlic, seared over open flame, and topped with house-made vibrant green chimichurri. Served with crispy tostones and yuca fries.

Mofongo con Camarones al Ajillo $24

Mashed fried green plantains seasoned with garlic, olive oil, and crispy chicharrón cracklings, crowned with succulent jumbo shrimp sautéed in white wine garlic butter sauce.

Pescado Frito al Estilo Boca Chica $25

Whole red snapper scored, seasoned with Dominican adobo and lime, fried until skin is shatteringly crisp while meat stays flaky. Served with tostones, lime wedges, and side green salad.

Bistec Encebollado $19

Thin-sliced tender sirloin steak marinated in sour orange and garlic, pan-seared with a mountain of sweet caramelized white onions and bell peppers in savory pan gravy.

Picaderas • Island Appetizers & Street Bites

Ideal for table sharing alongside craft cocktails and ice-cold beers.

Tostones con Salami Frito y Queso Blanco $13

Crispy twice-fried green plantain rounds served with grilled thick slices of Dominican Induveca salami, golden pan-fried Caribbean white cheese, fresh avocado, and house mayo-ketchup.

Chicharrón de Pollo Sin Hueso $12

Crispy bite-sized pieces of marinated chicken thighs fried golden with Dominican spices, tossed with pickled red onions and served with fresh lime wedges.

Empanadas Caseras (3pc) $10

Handmade flaky pastry turnovers stuffed with your choice of seasoned shredded chicken, seasoned ground beef picadillo, or melted Caribbean cheese.

Yuca Frita con Mojo de Ajo $9

Tender cassava root batons fried crisp on the outside, creamy on the inside, accompanied by warm garlic-citrus mojo dipping sauce.

Bebidas & Tropical Mixology

Crafted with premium Caribbean rums, fresh mint, and island fruit purées.

The República Signature Coco Mojito $14

Brugal Añejo Rum, cream of coconut, freshly squeezed lime juice, muddled mint leaves, simple syrup, and topped with fizzy club soda and toasted coconut rim.

Passion Fruit Caipirinha $14

Cachaça rum, fresh passion fruit (chinola) purée, muddled lime wedges, and raw cane sugar shaken over crushed pebble ice.

Artisanal Mamajuana Shot / Bottle $8 / $65

Traditional Dominican medicinal spirit infused with endemic tree barks, herbs, Dominican rum, red wine, and pure honey.

Cerveza Presidente Bien Fría $6

The iconic Dominican pilsner beer served with frosted "vestida de novia" ice crust on the bottle with fresh lime.
