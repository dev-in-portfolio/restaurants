## Original Audit Weakness

República Restaurant & Lounge relies heavily on scattered social media links and third-party delivery listings. As the Charlotte-Matthews metro's landmark destination for authentic Dominican cuisine by day and premier Latin nightlife lounge by night, it lacked an owned web presence conveying its culinary heritage (Sancocho, La Bandera, Tostones con Salami), scratch tropical cocktails, weekend DJ sets, and VIP bottle service hospitality.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Independence Boulevard Location:** 11315 E Independence Blvd, Matthews NC 28105. Positioned along the East Independence corridor with convenient parking and expansive indoor dining & lounge floor plan.
2. **Dominican & Caribbean Culinary Program:** Sancocho de res y víveres (simmered beef & root vegetable stew), La Bandera Dominicana (stewed meats, white rice, red beans, sweet plantains), Churrasco con chimichurri, Mofongo con camarones, Tostones con salami frito y queso frito, Coco Mojitos, Mamajuana reserve, and chilled Presidente beer.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 246-8073; Open Daily: 11:00 AM – 2:00 AM. Kitchen open daily for lunch and dinner; late-night Latin lounge transition with live DJs and bottle service.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Caribbean Amber Gold & Island Palm Green Palette:** High-energy tropical warmth anchored in deep night navy (`#080f1a`), rich palm emerald (`#064e3b`), vibrant Dominican amber gold (`#f59e0b`), coral terracotta (`#ea580c`), and crisp linen white (`#f8fafc`).
2. **Contemporary Latin Display Typography:** Bold modern geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) with high-contrast display weights, paired with clean readable body typography and tropical festival monospace metadata tags.
3. **Authentic Caribbean Photography:** Traditional silver caldero of rich Sancocho stew with bone-in beef and corn, crispy twice-fried tostones with Dominican salami and fried cheese, and a sparkling tall tropical craft cocktail on a dark mahogany bar.

### Divergent Choices for República Restaurant & Lounge

- **Hero Composition:** Split-hero layout featuring a República Caribbean Heritage Kicker (`"DOMINICAN CULINARY HERITAGE • LATIN NIGHTLIFE LOUNGE • MATTHEWS NC"`), vibrant amber CTA buttons, and a framed hero image of steaming Sancocho in a traditional silver caldero, anchored by a floating hero badge (`Independence Blvd | 11315 E Independence Blvd • Matthews NC`).
- **Section Rhythm:** Three-card standards grid without emojis highlighting "Authentic Dominican Gastronomy", "Tropical Mixology & Island Spirits", and "High-Energy Latin Sound & VIP Lounge", followed by two rich alternating highlight banners.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Bespoke classes (`republica-header`, `republica-brand`, `republica-hero-stage`, `republica-hero-badge`, `republica-standards-section`, `republica-standard-card`, `republica-highlight-banner`, `republica-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
