## Original Audit Weakness

Zapata's Mexican Restaurant is an established Charlotte institution famous for its scenic lakefront boardwalk patio in University Place (8927 J.M. Keynes Dr., Suite 900, Charlotte, NC 28262) and its Lake Norman sister location in Cornelius. Celebrated for sizzling cast-iron Fajitas Texanas ($22.99), signature jalapeño cheese dip ($7.99), tableside guacamole ($9.99), super nachos ($14.99), and jumbo cantina margaritas, its current web presence relies on a generic SpotHopper template with hidden navigation drawers, unstyled text walls, missing dedicated pages for its lakefront patio dining and catering packages, and cluttered iframe widgets.
