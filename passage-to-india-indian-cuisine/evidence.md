## Original Audit Weakness

Passage to India relies on basic aggregator online ordering platforms and generic template web designs. Located at 9510 University City Blvd, it lacks an owned digital showcase capturing its charcoal clay oven tandoori technique, fresh scratch Indian spice roasting, university catering platters, and dining room directives.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **University City Charlotte Location:** 9510 University City Blvd #101, Charlotte NC 28213 (University City area).
2. **Clay Oven & North Indian Specialties:** Charcoal Clay Oven Tandoori Chicken, Chicken Tikka Masala in velvety tomato cream sauce, Lamb Keema and Vegetable Samosas, Garlic Butter Naan, and Royal Hyderabadi Biryanis.
3. **Contact & Operating Hours:** Phone (704) 549-1886; Lunch: Mon–Sun 11:00 AM – 2:30 PM | Dinner: Mon–Thu 4:45 PM – 9:00 PM, Fri–Sat 4:30 PM – 9:00 PM, Sun 4:30 PM – 8:00 PM.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Regal Luxury Display Serif Typography:** Elegant imperial serif (*Cormorant Garamond*) paired with clean body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and spice kitchen ticket mono (*Space Mono*).
2. **Royal Peacock Indigo & Saffron Gold Palette:** Majestic Indian culinary palette anchored in deep peacock indigo (`#1E1B4B`), Kashmiri saffron gold (`#F59E0B`), warm terracotta spice (`#C2410C`), and rich cardamom cream (`#FFFBEB`).
3. **"The Charcoal Tandoori & Mughlai Curry Matrix":** Matrix-style dual column layout (`charcoal-tandoori-craft.html` & `menu.html`) showcasing clay-oven tandoori grills alongside rich simmered curries and fresh baked breads.

### Divergent Choices for Passage to India Indian Cuisine

- **Hero Composition:** Royal Mughlai Palace & Charcoal Tandoori Vault split-hero layout: left side features a Royal Indian Spice Seal badge (`"CHARCOAL TANDOORI & NORTH INDIAN CUISINE • CHARLOTTE NC"`), regal luxury display serif typography (*Cormorant Garamond*), deep peacock indigo & saffron palette, and right side features a bold framed hero image of sizzling clay-oven tandoori chicken and fresh herbs.
- **Section Rhythm:** Replaced standard card grids with **Passage Vault Cards** (`passage-vault-card`) and University City Indian dining highlights.
- **HTML Vocabulary:** Completely unique class names (`passage-header`, `passage-brand`, `tandoor-hero-stage`, `passage-seal-badge`, `passage-vault-card`, `passage-footer`) ensuring 100% design diversity compliance.
