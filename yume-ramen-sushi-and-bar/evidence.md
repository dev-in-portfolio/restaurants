## Original Audit Weakness

YUME Ramen Sushi & Bar is one of Charlotte’s most critically acclaimed Japanese culinary destinations, situated at 1508 S Mint Street in the historic Wilmore neighborhood adjacent to South End. Founded by Chef Tony Zheng and Michiko Zheng, YUME is celebrated for its 24-hour slow-simmered Tonkotsu and Spicy Miso ramen broths, melt-in-your-mouth braised chashu pork belly, ajitsuke tamago ramen eggs, hand-pressed nigiri and sashimi moriawase, crispy pan-seared pork gyoza, and Japanese whisky cocktails. Despite earning numerous local "Best Ramen" and "Best Sushi" awards, search engines frequently fail to showcase its dual lunch and dinner service hours or split its culinary profile into disconnected third-party listings, obscuring its full izakaya bar, sake pairings, and weekend continuous afternoon dining.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Kyoto Sumi Ink & Hinoki Wood Palette:** Deep Japanese sumi ink (`#121315`), charred binchotan charcoal (`#191b1f`), warm hinoki cedar (`#c99a5b`), vermilion red (`#c94132`), ceramic bone white (`#f7f5f0`), and soft bamboo mist (`#cfc9be`).
2. **Contemporary Japanese Typography:** Clean architectural serif (*Cinzel* / *Playfair Display*) paired with sharp geometric sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and izakaya ticket monospace (*DM Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A ceramic stoneware bowl of black garlic tonkotsu ramen with braised chashu pork, molten ajitsuke tamago, pickled ginger, and green scallions; a pristine white ceramic sushi platter with hand-pressed nigiri, avocado uramaki roll, and fresh wasabi; and pan-seared pork gyoza dumplings with crispy golden lace bottoms.
