## Original Audit Weakness

Tropical Goodies is a cornerstone culinary establishment located at 2316 Lasalle Street in Charlotte’s Historic West End. Renowned throughout Mecklenburg County for its authentic Jamaican and Caribbean soul food fusion—including slow-braised oxtails with butter beans, smoky scotch bonnet jerk chicken, tender curry goat, jerk salmon rasta pasta, fried whole wings in Tropical Goodies signature sauce, sweet fried plantains, and Southern baked mac & cheese—Tropical Goodies is celebrated for authentic island flavor. However, online directories often confuse its primary Lasalle Street flagship with its secondary Tuckaseegee Road location or list fragmented operating hours that understate its full daily lunch, dinner, and Sunday service.

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Physical Address:** 2316 Lasalle St, Charlotte, NC 28216 (Historic West End / West Charlotte).
2. **Contact Phone:** (704) 399-5036.
3. **Weekly Operating Schedule:** Monday – Thursday 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM; Friday – Saturday 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM; Sunday 12:00 PM – 8:00 PM.
4. **Authentic Jamaican Staples:** Slow-braised oxtails with butter beans and brown gravy, char-grilled scotch bonnet jerk chicken, curry goat with roti, and jerk rasta pasta.
5. **Caribbean Soul Fusion Sides:** Sweet golden fried plantains, coconut milk rice & peas, Southern baked macaroni & cheese, candied yams, and Jamaican sorrel ginger drinks.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Kingston Spice Charcoal & Turmeric Sun Palette:** Deep allspice charcoal (`#161413`), charred wood bark (`#1f1b18`), golden turmeric amber (`#d9822b`), warm scotch bonnet orange (`#e05a2b`), coconut cream (`#faf6ee`), and lush island palm (`#2a5a3b`).
2. **Bold Caribbean Typography:** Rich characterful serif (*Playfair Display* / *Cinzel*) paired with warm friendly sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and kitchen order monospace (*DM Mono*).
3. **Verified Culinary Photography:** A massive jerk chicken leg platter with charred jerk bark, scotch bonnet marinade, rice & peas, and fried plantains; slow-braised tender Jamaican oxtails with creamy butter beans in rich brown gravy; and a traditional golden curried goat platter with warm folded roti flatbread and fresh cabbage slaw.
