## Original Audit Weakness

Tommy's Pub is a historic Charlotte institution founded in 1977 by Tommy Sossamon on Central Avenue, now located at 3124 Eastway Drive, Suite 710 in Eastway Crossing under owner Jamie Starks. Celebrated as Charlotte's authentic live music dive bar, honky tonk, and home of the weekly Thursday open mic jam, its current online footprint lacks a dedicated high-performance standalone website, relying only on fragmented Facebook posts with no structured music calendar, no drink menu directory, and no clear location guide.

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual / Content Anchors

1. **Eastway Crossing Location:** 3124 Eastway Drive, Suite 710, Charlotte, NC 28205.
2. **Founding & Ownership:** Established in 1977 by Tommy Sossamon; owned and operated by Jamie Starks.
3. **Weekly Operating Hours:**
   - Monday – Thursday: 4:00 PM – 2:00 AM
   - Friday – Saturday: 2:00 PM – 2:00 AM
   - Sunday: 2:00 PM – 12:00 AM
4. **Authentic Photography Assets:**
   - `tommys-pub-stage.jpg`: Authentic interior photo of the horseshoe bar, NASCAR memorabilia, Dale Jr neons, and local patrons.
   - `jamie-starks-owner.jpg`: High-resolution portrait of owner Jamie Starks behind the pub bar with Murphy's Stout cans and specials board.
5. **Core Offerings:**
   - Free Live Music Showcases (Garage Rock, Outlaw Country, Punk, Blues)
   - Iconic Thursday Open Mic Jam Night
   - Cold $3 PBR / Domestic Cans, Murphy's Irish Stout, NC Craft Ales, and Well Whiskey Shots.

### Core Design Moves

1. **Honky Tonk Crimson & Neon Amber Palette:** Deep Velvet Charcoal (`#110f0e`), Weathered Stage Oak (`#1a1614`), Honky Tonk Crimson (`#9e2a2b`), Neon Amber Gold (`#e09f3e`), and Warm Tavern Cream (`#f8f4ec`).
2. **Vintage Americana Display Typography:** Display serif (*Rye* / *Playfair Display*) paired with clean geometric body sans (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) and tavern ticket monospace (*Space Mono*).
3. **Floating Hero Badge:** Concrete operational facts (`Tommy's Pub | 3124 Eastway Dr • Est. 1977 Charlotte`).
4. **Editorial Layout:** Three-card standards grid without emojis, followed by dual alternating highlight banners (`.tommys-highlight-banner`, `.reverse`).
