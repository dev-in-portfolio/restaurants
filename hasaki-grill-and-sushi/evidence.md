## Original Audit Weakness

Hasaki Grill is an Uptown Charlotte staple in the Ally Center, celebrated for its fast, fresh teppanyaki hibachi and signature sushi rolls for corporate professionals and stadium attendees. However, their web presence lacked a comprehensive, modern interactive showcase for corporate bento lunch orders, sushi roll ingredients, and gameday takeout options. This 6-page concept delivers a sharp, modern digital presence tailored to Uptown dining.

---

## Creative Brief

### Verified Visual & Content Anchors

1. **Sizzling Hibachi Combos:** Teppanyaki-grilled NY strip steak with mushrooms, Atlantic salmon with ginger glaze, jumbo shrimp, wok-tossed egg fried rice, sweet carrots, and signature white sauce.
2. **Signature Uptown Sushi Rolls:** Charlotte Panther Roll (spicy tuna, avocado, tempura crunch, eel sauce), Volcano Roll (baked spicy crab and scallop over California roll), and Rainbow Roll.
3. **Executive Bento Lunches & Starters:** Teriyaki chicken bento with gyoza dumplings, 4-piece California roll, seaweed salad, and steamed rice.

### Three Core Design Moves

1. **Hasaki Crimson & Charcoal Palette:** Hasaki Crimson (`#b91c1c`), Ink Charcoal (`#111827`), Matcha Green (`#4d7c0f`), and Bamboo Silk Cream (`#fbf9f5`).
2. **Japanese Modern Layout:** Clean minimalist cards, hibachi combo builders, specialty roll matrices, and express lunch takeaway callouts.
3. **Contemporary Typography:** Outfit display typography paired with Plus Jakarta Sans and DM Mono for clear sushi piece counts and corporate lunch rates.
